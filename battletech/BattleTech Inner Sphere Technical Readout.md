## ABOUT THIS READOUT

This document catalogs BattleMech designs, transport doctrine, and Successor State forces of the Inner Sphere as of 3025. Star League-era specifications are reproduced where surviving copies permit; much has been lost.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **3025 CE, Fourth Succession War brewing**: five Great Houses, centuries of lostech, and a ComStar that controls every faster-than-light message in human space.

## PART ONE — THE INNER SPHERE

### 1.0 Introduction

Humanity spread across a thousand worlds under the Star League, then destroyed that golden age in one coup. Three Succession Wars later, the Five Great Houses fight with regressing technology over ruins, while ComStar quietly owns every message between the stars.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Inner Sphere, ~600 light-year radius around Terra |
| Powers | Five Successor States: Federated Suns (Davion), Draconis Combine (Kurita), Lyran Commonwealth (Steiner), Free Worlds League (Marik), Capellan Confederation (Liao) |
| Neutral power | ComStar: Keeper of the HPG network, based on Terra |
| Warfare standard | BattleMech duels governed by the Ares Conventions |

Defining facts:

1. **Lostech is real.** Each war destroys more Star League knowledge than universities can rebuild. A working Star League-era laser is a treasure.
2. **JumpShips are sacrosanct.** Attacking one invites total retaliation; all strategy flows through fixed jump points at stars' zenith and nadir.
3. **BattleMechs decide everything.** A single Mech can take or hold a world the old way: by standing on it.

## PART TWO — TRANSPORT CHAIN: JUMPSHIPS AND DROPSHIPS

### 2.0 JumpShip Operations

**In brief:** Interstellar travel belongs to JumpShips: fragile sail-equipped vessels that charge at a star's zenith or nadir jump point, then translate up to thirty light-years per jump. They never fight. K-F drive recharge takes roughly a week with a solar sail.

| Property | Value **[E]** |
|---|---|
| Jump range | 30 light-years per jump |
| Recharge | ~175 hours (sail) |
| Collar capacity | 4 DropShips typical |
| Doctrine | Never jump close to gravity wells; never fight |

**DropShips** carry the war: Union-class (12 Mechs + fighters) and Overlord-class (36 Mechs) assault birds deliver lances from jump point to planetfall in days.

### 2.1 Hyperpulse Generators (HPG)

ComStar's HPG stations beam instant messages between worlds on a paid schedule. ComStar owns the network; ComStar reads the mail; ComStar interdicts worlds that annoy it. Every Successor Lord tolerates this because nobody can replace it.

### 6.1 The Five Successor Houses

| House | State | Character |

| Kurita | Draconis Combine | Samurai-coded honor culture; elite swordsmen regiments |

| Marik | Free Worlds League | Fractious parliament; good warships |
| Liao | Capellan Confederation | Smallest, most paranoid; Warrior Houses as fanatical elites |

### 6.2 ComStar

The semi-religious order running Terra and every HPG. Publicly: neutral communicators. Actually: hoarding Star League caches, playing houses against each other, waiting for the day interstellar civilization needs its only messenger to become its only master **[W]**.

### 6.3 Mercenaries

Wolf's Dragoons and a thousand lesser commands sell lances by the contract. The Ares Conventions civilize the trade: no atrocity without consequence, ransoms honored, wars scheduled. Break the Conventions and every mercenary house declines your next contract.

## PART EIGHT — BESTIARY OF THE SUCCESSION WARS

### 8.0 Lostech

**What:** The steady disappearance of capability: each Succession War burns libraries and shipyards.
**Cost of contact:** Your grandfather's mech was better than yours, and nobody alive knows why it worked.

### 8.2 JumpPoint Interdiction

**What:** Pirates ambush DropShip convoys arriving at recharge points.
**Cost of contact:** Every arrival is an unguarded moment; escorts exist for this reason.

### 8.3 ComStar Interdiction

**What:** HPG blackout as diplomatic weapon: worlds cut off from the galaxy until they comply.
**Cost of contact:** Weeks of silence that end governments.
