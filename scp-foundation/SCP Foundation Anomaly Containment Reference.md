**SECURE · CONTAIN · PROTECT**

## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This reference consolidates containment doctrine, anomalous-object classification, and organizational structure for Foundation personnel. It supplements field judgment, this setting's drama is ordinary people maintaining an extraordinary lie at any cost, and every procedure here exists because someone once failed to follow it.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **the modern containment era** (Site-19 operational, MTF network matured, amnestic standardization complete). The Foundation does not date its documents by calendar year; continuity is maintained by revision number.

**Conventions used throughout:**

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(no marker)* | Verified per Foundation records |
| **[E]** | Extrapolated procedure detail; site variance applies |
| **[W]** | Writer's latitude note |

Where this reference conflicts with a containment procedure on file, **the procedure wins**, and this document is wrong.

## PART ONE: THE VEIL

### 1.0 Introduction

The Foundation exists to Secure anomalous objects and entities, to Contain them where destruction or explanation would damage normalcy, and to Protect civilian populations from a truth the species is not prepared to hold. We are not scientists first, soldiers second, or bureaucrats third. We are the masquerade's stage crew.

**In brief:** Every anomaly contained is a war never fought over a truth nobody can afford.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Global; off-world sites exist under separate charter |
| Infrastructure | 100+ Sites and Areas worldwide **[E]**; Site-19 is the largest containment facility |
| Personnel | Researchers, security, containment specialists, Class-D expendables, Mobile Task Forces |
| Posture | Perpetual secrecy ("the Veil") against public knowledge *and* rival organizations |

Three facts define all operations:

1. **Normalcy is load-bearing.** Human consensus reality constrains many anomalies (see §8.2). Widespread belief in the anomalous can *strengthen* it. Secrecy is physics, not politics.
2. **Everything is containable until it isn't.** Classification reflects containment difficulty, never threat dismissed.
3. **The Foundation contains; it does not explain.** Understanding is pursued only when it improves containment.

**[W]** SCP stories run on procedure failing gracefully or catastrophically. Write the procedure first, then break it honestly.
### 1.2 Object Classification System

| Class | Meaning | Containment posture |
|---|---|---|
| **Safe** | Reliably containable by known procedure | Lock it in a box; it stays |
| **Euclid** | Unpredictable; procedure requires active maintenance or vigilance | Watch the box |
| **Keter** | Containment is difficult, resource-intensive, or fails regularly | The box is a battlefield |
| **Thaumiel** | Anomalies used to contain *other* anomalies | The Foundation's own arsenal — classified above most clearance |
| **Apollyon** | Containment impossible; world-ending trajectory acknowledged | Prepare for failure |
| **Neutralized** | No longer anomalous | Archive |

**Secondary classifications:** *Uncontained* (location unknown), *Esoteric* classes by site charter, *Explained* (anomaly retired to normal science).

### 1.3 Personnel Classification

- **Level 0–2:** Public-facing and clerical staff; know only their fragment.
- **Level 3:** Security researchers and senior staff; know project details.
- **Level 4:** Site directors and command staff.
- **Level 5 / O5 Council:** The twelve who govern. Nobody at Level 4 has ever met all of them **[W]**.
- **Class-D ("Expendable"):** Death-row recruits used for direct anomaly interaction. Monthly termination-and-replacement was early doctrine; current policy rotates via amnestic wipe where feasible **[E]**.

### 1.5 Status Quo Snapshot

| Domain | Status |
|---|---|
| Sites | Global network operational; Site-19 holds 400+ objects **[E]** |
| Veil integrity | Holding; amnestic coverage near-total |
| Rival organizations | Active (§6.x): GOC "destroy-first" doctrine vs. Foundation containment remains the central philosophical schism |
| Keter roster | Stable but never shrinking |
| Reality stability | Within tolerances; Scranton anchor network expanding |

## PART TWO: SITE-19

### 2.0 Site Overview

**In brief:** The Foundation's largest containment facility: hundreds of Safe/Euclid objects under one roof, research wings, MTF staging, and enough institutional weight that "Site-19" is shorthand for the Foundation itself.

| Property | Value **[E]** |
|---|---|
| Objects contained | 400+ |
| Personnel | Thousands across shifts |
| Structure | Subterranean containment wings + surface administrative campus |
| Special assets | Humanoidoid containment wing, memetics division, autonomous contingency systems |

```
   SITE-19 — SECTION CUT (schematic)

   [SURFACE CAMPUS] admin · motor pool · helo pad
   ================================ bedrock line
   [-10] humanoid containment wing
   [-20] Euclid object vaults (specialized per-object)
   [-30] Safe storage · research labs
   [-40] MTF staging · armory · Class-D housing
   [-50] ON-SITE AMNESTIC RESERVE · failover core
        [blast doors between every level]
```

### 2.1 Containment Wing Doctrine

Every object gets bespoke containment built to its procedure, there is no universal cell. Standard fittings: layered blast doors, independent environmental systems, observation galleries behind blast glass, and kill/flush infrastructure sized to the object's worst documented day.

**[W]** A containment breach story is an engineering audit performed by survivors. Name the exact procedure step that failed; the horror is procedural before it is physical.

## PART THREE: CONTAINMENT OPERATIONS

### 3.1 Procedure Architecture

Every anomaly file follows the standard skeleton: **Item # → Object Class → Special Containment Procedures → Description**. Addenda record breaches, experiments, and interview logs.

- Procedures are written by the object's lead researcher, ratified at Level 3+, and amended after *every* incident.
- "Procedure" is law aboard Foundation ground: deviation requires O5 or Ethics Committee waiver in writing.
- The Ethics Committee exists, has real teeth, and approves the terrible things so they happen under rules **[W]**.

### 3.2 Amnestic Program

| Class | Strength | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Class A | Total identity rewrite | Witnessed an Apollyon event |
| Class B | Recent-memory erasure | Standard civilian exposure |
| Class C | Targeted erasure | Long-term infiltration cleanup |
| Class D/E | Light suppression / milder | Routine field hygiene |

Delivery: aerosol, injection, ingestible. Misfires produce vegetative states, dosage discipline saves lives and reputations.

### ITEM #: SCP-173

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** Item is to be contained within a locked containment cell at all times. Personnel entering are to be briefed and enter in teams of no fewer than three; at least two must maintain direct eye contact with the item until the door is resealed. On any creak or movement within the cell, all personnel are to blink in sequence, never simultaneously.

**Description:** A concrete-and-rebar sculpture animate when line of sight is broken. Movement is instantaneous between observed states. Contact results in death by cervical fracture, followed by [DATA EXPUNGED] from the resulting orifice.

**Addendum, Site-19 incident excerpt:** *"...the two-minute gap in the camera record corresponds exactly to the janitorial staff's coffee break. We do not have a staffing problem. We have a blinking problem."* **[W]**

---

### ITEM #: SCP-096

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** Item contained in a 5m × 5m × 5m airtight steel cube. No imagery of the face may exist in any medium; web-scrub task forces (MTF Zeta-? under Panopticon oversight) destroy reproductions on discovery. Viewing protocols require personnel to avoid the facial region even in peripheral vision.

**Description:** A pale, emaciated humanoid, 2.38 m tall. When its face is viewed, photographically or directly, the subject enters distress, covers its face, then enters pursuit state: it will travel any distance, through any obstacle, killing the viewer. No force tested has stopped it mid-pursuit.

**Addendum, retrieval interview excerpt (redacted):** *"You didn't look at it. You looked at a picture of it. It doesn't care about the difference, and neither should you."*

---

### ITEM #: SCP-682

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** Item contained in a 5 m acid immersion tank within a 25 m chamber lined with acid-resistant plating. Feeding occurs via chute. Any personnel entering the chamber require Level 4 authorization, full hazmat, and an O5 counter-signature.

**Description:** Large reptilian organism of unknown origin; intelligent, adaptive, and vocal about its contempt for all life. Regenerates from ~1% body mass. Termination attempts (see Incident log: 682-C15 through -C68) have repeatedly enhanced it.

**Addendum:** *"It adapts. Every attempt to end it becomes a lesson it keeps. Recommend we stop teaching."*, Ethics Committee memo, unheeded **[W]**

---

### ITEM #: SCP-049

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** Humanoid containment suite styled as a 16th-century plague doctor's quarters; interaction by appointment. Subject cooperative if permitted to continue its "work", which is not permitted.

**Description:** Humanoid entity in plague physician garb (garb and body indistinguishable under imaging). Touch is lethal to humans; subjects subsequently reanimate as mindless 049-2 instances. The entity is courteous, articulate, and convinced it is curing a disease only it can perceive.

**Interview excerpt:** *"Good Doctor, your kind calls it murder. I call it the cure arriving before the understanding. History will thank us both, one of us posthumously."*

---

### ITEM #: SCP-914

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** Contained in a climate-controlled 20×20 m room with observation gallery. Testing requires Level 2 sign-off; Very Fine settings require Level 3.

**Description:** Clockwork refinement machine of ~18 m² footprint, comprising an Input booth, Output booth, and a selector dial: **Rough → Coarse → 1:1 → Fine → Very Fine**. Insertion of objects produces refined outputs per inscrutable internal logic.

**Selected test-log extracts:**

| Setting | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | 1 kg steel ingot | 1 kg steel shavings |
| Fine | Wristwatch | Better wristwatch |
| Very Fine | Wristwatch | Wristwatch that runs slightly ahead of time. Locally. |
| Rough | Human cadaver | **[DATA EXPUNGED]** — testing suspended |

---

### ITEM #: SCP-106

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** Suspended in a magnetically-levitated containment cell within a lead-lined shell; no solid contact with any surface permitted. Cell breach triggers Protocol 106-Primrose: lure-and-recapture using juvenile human bait **[W]**, Ethics Committee waiver renewed annually over standing objection.

**Description:** Elderly humanoid entity whose passage corrodes matter ("corrosion trails"); phases through solids; drags victims into a pocket dimension of uncertain physics from which return is rare, delayed decades, and psychologically total.

## PART SIX: GROUPS OF INTEREST

*Organizations aware of the anomalous. Relations ratings follow Foundation liaison shorthand: positive, neutral, hostile, or hostile-with-overlap.*

### 6.1 Global Occult Coalition (GOC)

**In brief:** United Nations-chartered paramilitary body dedicated to destruction of anomalous objects. Where the Foundation contains, the GOC destroys.

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Structure | UN oversight council + operational strike teams ("PHYSICS division") |
| Doctrine | Anomalies are threats to be eliminated, cataloged only after termination |
| Relations with Foundation | Hostile-with-overlap: shared targets, opposed methods |
| Notable doctrine | The Seventh Standard: no use of anomalous assets, ever |

**Friction point:** GOC destruction of an object the Foundation wants preserved triggers joint-custody standoffs. Their track record includes at least one destroyed object whose termination released something worse **[W]**.

### 6.2 Chaos Insurgency

**In brief:** Former Foundation defectors (the 1924 split) who kept the research and discarded the ethics. They weaponize anomalies instead of containing them, and their cells poach Foundation personnel and inventory by name.

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Origin | Defection of Site-? research elements during the "Insurgency" schism |
| Doctrine | Capture, exploit, sell |
| Relations | Hostile. Active targeting of Foundation assets |

Their knowledge of Foundation procedure makes them the most dangerous human adversary on file: they know our playbook because they wrote part of it.

### 6.3 Serpent's Hand

**In brief:** Decentralized movement asserting the anomalous should be free and public. Operates from the Wanderers' Library (see §8.x), which the Foundation cannot enter by force without consequences nobody can model.

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Structure | Cell-less ideology; no known leadership to capture |
| Doctrine | Liberation of anomalous objects and disclosure to the public |
| Relations | Hostile to Veil enforcement generally; occasionally aligned against worse actors |

**[W]** Serpent's Hand arguments are written to be persuasive. A story where they are simply wrong misses the point of them.

### 6.4 Marshall, Carter & Dark Ltd.

**In brief:** Exclusive club brokering anomalous experiences and objects to the wealthy. They do not breach containment; they purchase inventory through channels we then have to buy back.

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Doctrine | Commerce. Everything has a price list |
| Relations | Neutral-hostile. Litigation-grade rather than violent |

### 6.5 Church of the Broken God

**In brief:** Machine-worship faith seeking reassembly of their broken deity (Mekhane). Anomalous cybernetics, clockwork constructs, and a theology of replacing flesh. Factional splits (Breakers vs. reconstructionists) matter more than the Church's unified front suggests.

### 6.6 Horizon Initiative

Abrahamic-faith umbrella organization claiming religious authority over anomalies. Smaller footprint, significant reach inside faith communities, occasional cooperation on shared containment.

### 6.7 Anderson Robotics

Anomalous robotics and AI vendor. Products circulate through Marshall Carter & Dark channels; their full android line remains unrecovered **[E]**.

### 6.8 Wilson's Wildlife Solutions

Bellingham-area wildlife control that keeps encountering low-grade anomalies and handling them with disarming competence and paperwork the Foundation keeps having to launder. Cooperative. Bring donuts **[E]**.

## PART EIGHT: PHENOMENA BESTIARY

### 8.1 Memetics and Antimemetics

**What:** Memetics: information that behaves like a pathogen, ideas that alter behavior on perception. Kill-agents are offensive memetics. Antimemetics: information that resists being remembered at all; antimemetic anomalies are contained *by forgetting*, which is also how containment staff get lost.
**Where:** Memetics Division, Site-? **[W]**.
**Cost of contact:** You cannot trust your own notes. Antimemetics Division staff leave themselves letters.

### 8.2 Reality Benders

**What:** Individuals or objects that alter consensus reality directly. Type classifications (Blue/Green/Black) scale by power and control.
**Where:** Anywhere. Consensus reality is the battlefield.
**Cost of contact:** Reality benders at scale invalidate containment physics. SRAs level the field; nothing levels it fairly.

### 8.3 Pataphysics **[W]**

**What:** The study of narrative itself as an anomalous force, the hypothesis that our reality is written. Departmental access only.
**Cost of contact:** Departments that research pataphysics report higher rates of authors' block. Draw no conclusions.

### 8.4 Temporal Anomalies

**What:** Loops, reversals, divergent futures, and time-traveling objects. Foundation policy: preserve the timeline unless already broken, in which case document and contain the breakage.
**Cost of contact:** Causality debt. Every fix borrows against a future nobody has audited.
