# THE EXPANSE: TECHNICAL MANUAL

**SOL SYSTEM OPERATIONS REFERENCE, FIRST EDITION**

*Eighth entry in the collected set. Prepared in the tradition of the Star Trek Writers'/DS9 Technical Manuals: an in-universe reference consolidating established canon, with clearly marked engineering extrapolation.*

## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This manual documents the technology and politics of the Sol System in the era of the protomolecule. It is hard science fiction: every system here obeys thermodynamics, and every miracle is paid for in delta-v, radiation dose, and politics.

**Edition pin.** This First Edition reflects status quo as of **the opening of the Ring (Sol gate) and the formation of the Ring blockade fleet** (see §1.5).

## PART ONE: THE THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

Humanity lives in one system and fights over it like the last island on Earth: Earth holds the gravity wells and the population; Mars holds the navy and the dream; the Belt holds everything else, air, water, ore, and grievances three generations deep. Then something ancient started rewriting the rules from Venus outward.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Sol System, all orbits |
| Blocs | UN (Earth/Luna), Martian Congressional Republic, Outer Planet Alliance (Belt) |
| Flashpoints | The Ring (Sol–Slow Zone gate), Ganymede, Tycho, Earth's coastlines |
| Posture | Three-way cold war turned hot, interrupted by first contact |

Defining facts:

1. **The high ground is real.** Dropping rocks is cheap; stopping them is not. Every treaty since exists to keep kinetic bombardment theoretical.
2. **The Belt breathes through filters.** Air, water, spin-gravity: Belters live inside machines their grandparents built. Politics follows life support.
3. **The protomolecule rewrote physics once already** (Eros) and opened a door nobody understands. First contact happened; nobody was consulted.

### 1.2 Consolidated Timeline

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| 19th–21st c. | Slow diaspora: Luna, Mars terraforming project, Belt mining corporations |
| 22nd–23rd c. | Earth-Mars cold war; Belt labor under Inners' corporate rule |
| Eros incident | Protomolecule (released on Phoebe→Eros by Protogen experiments) converts 100,000 people into a relativistic impactor aimed at Earth; diverted to Venus |
| Thoth/Anderson Station | Protogen conspiracy exposed; protomolecule samples secured |
| Ganymede incident | Marine firefight over specimen control; refugee crisis across Jovian moons |
| Venus conversion | Protomolecule builds a structure; launches the Ring at Uranus-orbit distance |
| Ring opening | 1,300+ worlds accessible beyond the gate. **Edition pin:** blockade fleet stationed |

### 1.3 Assessment of Powers

**UN (Earth & Luna).** Thirty billion people, naval supremacy in numbers, and a welfare state stretched to orbit. Earth wins any long war by existing.

**Mars.** A nation defined by its terraforming dream: smaller population, superior navy, stealth tech generations ahead. Martian marines train for Earth's beaches they may never walk.

**The OPA / Belt.** Not a government so much as a diaspora with grudges, dockworkers' unions turned navy (Tycho station as shipyard/flag), Belter creole as national language, and the void itself as homeland. Fred Johnson's moderation vs. Marco Inaros-style radicalism is the Belt's own civil war **[W]**.

**Protogen/Corporate actors.** The companies that tried to weaponize first contact. Profit logic applied to xenobiology; results classified as atrocities.
### 1.4 Founding Technologies

**In brief:** The system's politics ride on three machines: the Epstein drive (fusion torch that made the Belt habitable and the war portable), the PDC/railgun defensive-offensive pairing (combat as thermodynamics), and, since Eros, the protomolecule, a technology nobody built and everybody now guards.

### 1.5 Status Quo Snapshot (Ring Blockade Era)

| Domain | Status |
|---|---|
| The Ring | Open; Sol gate under combined UN/MCR/OPA blockade flotilla |
| Slow Zone | One explored system: 1,300+ dormant gates, a dead station, physics that caps velocities (§8.2) |
| Earth-Mars relations | Cold-war hot; UNN and MCRN fleets eyeballing each other across the Ring line |
| Belt | OPA legitimacy surging; Inners' colonial grip cracking permanently |
| Protomolecule | Sample stocks secured (Tycho); Venus structure inert post-Ring launch **[W]** |

**[W]** The Ring changes the genre from cold war to frontier western. Everything before the pin is politics; everything after is archaeology with guns.

## PART TWO: SHIPBOARD LIFE & THE ROCINANTE

### 2.0 The Rocinante

**In brief:** A Martian gunship (MCRN corvette class) stolen during the Ganymede incident, reflagged repeatedly, crewed by one of each of the system's peoples, the setting's argument for cooperation, in hull form.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Class | MCRN Corvette-class (light gunship) |
| Length | ~46 m **[E]** |
| Crew | 4–6 |
| Drive | Epstein fusion torch (see §2.2); high-gee capable with juice |
| Armament | 2 × torpedo launchers (plasma warheads), 2 × PDC turrets, railgun **[E]** |
| Defense | Reactive armor plating, point-defense network |

**Doctrine:** the Roci wins by being over-engineered Martian hardware flown by people who shouldn't be alive. High-g burns on "juice" (the cocktail) exceed sustained human tolerance briefly; every burn is a medical decision.

### 2.01 Rocinante Deck Zones

| Zone | Function |
|---|---|
| Ops/command deck | Cockpit, tactical station, crash couches rated for high-g burns |
| Mid-deck | Galley/mess (the crew's church), crew cabins, medbay with autodoc |
| Machine shop | Spare parts fabrication — the Roci stays alive here |
| Airlock/EVA | Vac suit storage, boarding gear, the airlock everyone distrusts post-incident |
| Cargo/ammo | Torpedo reloads, railgun slug storage, protomolecule sample safe **[W]** |

**[W]** Four seats, one ship, three governments' worth of flags she's worn. The Roci's registry changes; her crew doesn't. That constancy is the setting's emotional anchor, protect it in any story that touches the ship.

### 2.2 The Epstein Drive

**In brief:** The engine that made the setting: a fusion torch with efficiency so far beyond its inventor's design that he rode it until his fuel ran out. Sustained high-thrust burns shrink the solar system, weeks to Jupiter, months to Saturn, at ⅓g cruise and multi-g combat burns.

- **Burn math shapes every plot:** travel time = burn half-way, flip, decelerate. High-g saves days and breaks bodies.
- **Juice:** stimulant/protective cocktail allowing humans to endure combat gees; dosage has limits and invoices.

## PART THREE: COMMUNICATIONS AND SURVEILLANCE

Light-lag is law: Earth-to-Belt conversations run minutes behind; outer-system messages take hours. No FTL exists. Every political drama plays out over delayed video calls where silence is strategy. Media feeds are corporate/state-run (Trilogy News Network et al.), information warfare is literal broadcast warfare.

### 3.x Light-Lag Table (planning values)

| Link | One-way lag |
|---|---|
| Earth ↔ Moon | ~1.3 sec |
| Earth ↔ Mars (average) | 4–20 min |
| Earth ↔ Jupiter system | 33–53 min |
| Earth ↔ Saturn/Ring space | 70–90+ min |
| Inner-system ↔ Ring blockade fleet | Hours |

**[W]** Light-lag is the Expanse's dramatic engine: every negotiation scene has a built-in fuse, and no commander can rescue anyone in real time. Write the delay; don't tech it away.

**Combat doctrine (system-wide):**

- **PDCs (point-defense cannons):** rotary autocannons against torpedoes and close ships; the sound of any engagement's last minute.
- **Torpedoes:** nuclear-tipped or plasma; duels are torpedo-versus-PDC math plus railgun timing.
- **Railguns:** spinal mass drivers, the armor-piercing answer when barriers fail; firing solution measured in fractions of degrees.
- **Boarding:** rare, desperate, decided by Marines and vacuum discipline.

## PART FIVE: WEAPONS, EQUIPMENT, AND MATERIALS

### 5.1 Weapons

| System | Principle | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Railgun | Electromagnetic slug launch | Hull-splitting; spinal or turret mount; the decisive close-range weapon |
| Torpedoes (nuclear/plasma) | Guided kinetic/energy warheads | Long-range duels; PDC networks are their counter |
| PDC network | Rotary autocannons | Last-line defense; shreds torpedoes at knife range |
| Small arms | Conventional ballistics | Marines fight with rifles; vacuum-rated |

### 5.2 Life-Support and Belter Kit

- **Vac suits:** every Belt child drills suit failure before reading; seal discipline is culture.
- **Juice (high-g cocktail)** and **the chill** (torpor for long transits): chemistry as life support.
- **Air/water recyclers:** the Belt's true crown jewels; sabotage of recyclers is genocide by plumbing.

### 5.3 Strategic Materials

- **Water ice:** currency, fuel feedstock (reaction mass), life.
- **Metals from metallic asteroids:** shipbuilding stock; Ceres/Vesta industrial bases.
- **Protomolecule samples:** the most dangerous substance in existence, a self-assembling alien machine code that builds structures using biomass as components. Every gram is a casus belli.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 The Protomolecule

**In brief:** An alien agent of infrastructure: self-replicating nanotechnology that hijacks biology as construction material, then executes its builders' engineering plans, gates, stations, whatever the design requires. Not malevolent; *indifferent* at civilization-ending scale.

**More:**

- **Behavior:** infects biomass, integrates nervous systems (victims remain partially aware in the structures it builds), rewrites matter into alien machinery.
- **The Eros lesson:** given a city-sized sample, it moved a city-sized object at relativistic speed to build a gate. Treat any release as an extinction event with homework attached.
- **Containment:** Tycho station's secure labs hold samples under combined UN/MCR/OPA guard; research continues because whoever understands the gates controls thirteen hundred worlds.

**Engagement rules:** Never study it without kill-switch protocols. Never believe it is finished. Always ask what structure the outbreak is building.

#### Protomolecule Behavior Phases (observed)

| Phase | Behavior | Observed at |
|---|---|---|
| Infection | Hijacks biomass; victims partially aware | Phoebe/Eros |
| Integration | Converts matter into alien machinery; builds structures | Eros, Venus |
| Relativistic deployment | Moves massive objects to targets | Eros → Venus |
| Gate construction | Builds ring-gates linking star systems | Sol/Uranus distance |
| Dormancy/inertness | Post-construction shutdown; samples inert until triggered **[W]** | Tycho labs |

### 6.2 Free Navy Radicalism **[W]**

Inaros-style faction politics: asteroid bombardment of Earth as liberation strategy. The threat isn't ships, it's rocks on rails and the legitimacy they buy among desperate Belters.

### 6.3 Corporate Paramilitaries

Protogen-descended black ops: stealth hulls, deniable atrocities, shareholders who never face the people they experiment on.

## PART EIGHT: A SYSTEM BESTIARY

### 8.0 The Ring (Sol Gate)

**What:** A 1,000 km ring of inert protomolecule architecture past Uranus's orbit; transiting its center deposits ships in the Slow Zone.
**Where:** Wherever you are now.
**Cost of contact:** The universe got bigger and the neighborhood got worse.

### 8.2 The Slow Zone Station & Velocity Rule

**What:** A dead control station in a starless void containing 1,300 dormant gates; enforces a hard velocity cap on everything inside (≈ hundreds of m/s) via unexplained physics, violations are corrected lethally by the station itself.
**Where:** The hub between all gates.
**Cost of contact:** The station decides what "too fast" means. It has already decided.

### 8.3 Eros Relativistic Incident (historical)

**What:** A 337-km asteroid-city accelerated to ~5% c by converted human biomass, aimed at Earth, diverted to Venus at the last hour.
**Where:** Venus cloud decks now host the completed structure.
**Cost of contact:** Already paid: one hundred thousand people became infrastructure.

### 8.4 Radiation Environments

**What:** Jupiter's belts, solar storms, drive-plume exposure, the setting's honest everyday killer.
**Where:** Anywhere outside magnetospheres.
**Cost of contact:** Cancer with a ten-year fuse. Belters' physiology is adaptation written in tumors.
