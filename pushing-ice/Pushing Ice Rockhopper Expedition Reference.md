**LIND SALVAGE & EXPLORATION CHARTER — FIRST EDITION**

*Twenty-first volume in the collected set; house-style variant: authored as an expedition operations log for a single-ship charter. Canon derives from one novel; areas the source never explored are marked honestly rather than filled.*

## ABOUT THIS MANUAL

This reference documents the *Rockhopper* expedition: a comet-mining crew who chased a departing moon out of the solar system and became founders of humanity's interstellar diaspora. It supplements character drama; the source's central story is a friendship that breaks a crew and a timeline that breaks every promise home.

**Edition pin.** This manual reflects status quo as of **the settlement era following arrival and first Fountainhead contact**: the expedition stranded aboard Janus at its destination, Earth 260 years in the past-lightcone.

## PART ONE — THE DEEP-FIELD THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

In 2057 the crew of the *Rockhopper* pushed ice: herding comets back to human space for water, fuel, and profit. Then Saturn's moon Janus shed its disguise, accelerated past every probe humanity owned, and left the solar system under its own power. The Rockhopper was the only ship close enough to follow. Nobody told them the door would lock behind them.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Janus interior/settlement; origin Sol System 260 light-years and 260 years distant |
| Complement | Original crew ~40 **[E]** plus generations born en route |
| Powers (expedition) | Lind loyalists vs Barseghian faction vs neutral skilled trades |
| External powers | Fountainheads (benevolent, secretive), Musk Dogs (hostile empire), absent Spican builders |

Three facts define everything:

1. **The trip is one-way and was known to be.** Earth falsified fuel telemetry so the crew would commit. Trust in distant authority died first and stayed dead.
2. **Time runs differently for everyone else.** Thirteen years aboard equals centuries outside; every era they reach has forgotten their departure.
3. **Leadership legitimacy fractured on day one** and never fully healed: two founders, two philosophies, one population.

## PART THREE — CAPTURE AND TRANSIT OPERATIONS

### 3.0 The Wake-Field Capture

**In brief:** Janus projects a field that caught the Rockhopper during shadowing: acceleration matched, escape mathematically impossible. The crew's choice was never "go home or stay"; it was "land and live aboard, or drift dead in the black."

**Transit physics (observed):**

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Time dilation | 13 years ship-time ≈ 260 years Earth-frame at cruise |
| Wake capture | No fuel cost to the captured vessel; the field carries all |
| Course | Fixed: Spica approach corridor; no steering authority granted |

## PART FOUR — JANUS AND EXTERNAL CRAFT

### 4.1 Janus (Machine-Moon)

**In brief:** A 200-km-class **[E]** moon-shell that is an automated starship: ice camouflage over machine strata, interior cavities converted to habitat space for passengers it collected across deep time.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Class | Automated collector-vessel (Spican origin) |
| Automation | Full environmental maintenance; no crewed contact from builders ever |
| Defenses | Passive; ultimately destroyed by Musk Dog assault **[post-pin]** |
| Mission | Deliver gathered civilizations to a gathering structure |

**Interior doctrine:** the crew lands on the surface, lives under manufactured conditions, and never meets the owners. The ship is a bus with nobody driving.

### 4.2 Fountainhead Structures

Vast jellyfish-mountain organisms/structures at destination: benevolent, ancient, communicative on their own terms, masters of rejuvenation biotech. They share gifts sparingly and truths never fully.

### 4.3 Musk Dog Craft **[E]**

Hostile civilization's vessels: raiders who eventually overwhelm and destroy Janus. Their doctrine is domination of weaker gathered species.

## PART FIVE — EXPEDITION ARMAMENT AND EQUIPMENT

- **Mining nuclear devices:** one deployed to deny the abandoned comet to rivals; magazine retained as strategic reserve.
- **Cutting/boring gear:** industrial; doubles as construction equipment for settlement.
- **Vacuum-rated suits and heavy plant:** survival infrastructure.
- **Rejuvenation technology (Fountainhead gift):** biological age reset; transforms population demographics permanently.

**Strategic materials:** water-ice stockpiles, refined metals from refinery stores, and the irreplaceable human skill base: forty people's entire professional knowledge.

## PART SIX — THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.1 The Schism (internal)

**In brief:** The defining threat was never alien. Bella Lind's decision to land split the crew; Svetlana Barseghian's faction considered it kidnapping. Two competent leaders ran parallel power structures for years, reconciled by circumstance, ruptured again by principle.

**Engagement rules:** Never resolve the Lind/Barseghian rift permanently; its recurrence is the setting's engine. Every crisis re-litigates the original landing decision.

### 6.2 The Musk Dogs

An expansionist carnivorous civilization encountered in the gathering structure: brutal by temperament and empire by habit. They destroy Janus in the historical record. Their arrival forces former enemies into coalition **[W]**.

### 6.3 Institutional Deception (the standing threat)

Earth falsified telemetry. Bella withheld Earth's catastrophes from the crew. Fountainheads concealed the zoo truth. The pattern repeats at every scale: *information is rationed for your own good.* Every generation rediscovers a lie its founders lived inside.

## PART EIGHT — BESTIARY OF THE DEEP FIELD

### 8.0 The Iron Sky

**What:** A roof of automated construction raised over Janus before arrival: sky as hatch cover.
**Where:** Janus surface, destination system (actual location: millions of light-years off, see §8.2).
**Cost of contact:** It converts "sky" into a lid. Claustrophobia becomes cosmology.

### 8.2 The Gathering Structure ("Zoo")

**What:** A megastructure containing multiple transplanted civilizations, assembled by the Spicans because intelligent life almost never coexists in time. The guests are separated by epochs and light-years; the zoo is where extinct timelines meet.
**Cost of contact:** Every neighbor is a ghost of someone else's era. Nothing here is contemporary with anything.

### 8.3 Femtotech Cube

**What:** A black cube of morphable femtotechnology sent as aid by far-future humanity inspired by the Benefactor legend: godlike capability delivered as a thank-you note across eighteen millennia.
**Where:** Late-era settlement records.
**Cost of contact:** Responsibility without instructions.

### 8.4 Relativistic Estrangement

**What:** The standing condition: every year aboard is decades outside; everyone the crew knew is dead before arrival; new arrivals (none come) would be younger than the colony's grandchildren.
**Cost of contact:** Homesickness with no home left in the present tense.
