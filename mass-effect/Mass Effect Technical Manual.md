# MASS EFFECT: TECHNICAL MANUAL

**SYSTEMS ALLIANCE / CITADEL CIVILIZATION REFERENCE, FIRST EDITION**

*Fifth entry in the collected set. Prepared in the tradition of the Star Trek Writers'/DS9 Technical Manuals: an in-universe reference consolidating established canon, with clearly marked engineering extrapolation.*

---

## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This manual provides the detailed technical background that stories in the Milky Way of the Mass Effect era sometimes need. It supplements character work, never replaces it, this setting's drama is political and moral, carried by a physics that makes everyone's empire possible and nobody's safe.

**Edition pin.** This First Edition reflects status quo as of **2186 CE, the opening months of the Reaper invasion** (see §1.5). Endgame events after that point are deliberately unresolved here.

**Conventions used throughout:**

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(no marker)* | Established by game/codex canon |
| **[E]** | Extrapolation — internally consistent detail where canon was silent |
| **[W]** | Writer's latitude note |

Where this manual conflicts with established canon, canon wins. Log conflicts in Appendix C.

**Structure:** Part One sets the galactic theater. Part Two covers the Citadel and the Normandy. Part Three covers relay/FTL operations. Part Four carries vessel specifications. Part Five covers weapons, equipment, and strategic materials. Part Six is the threat dossier. Part Seven defines command structures. Part Eight catalogs phenomena. Appendices carry glossary, conversions, the canon log, and index.

## PART ONE: THE GALACTIC THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

Civilization in this galaxy runs on borrowed infrastructure: a relay network built fifty thousand years ago by an extinct people, terminating at a station that predates all living memory. Every species found its place in someone else's architecture, and the architects are coming home.

**In brief:** The setting's core irony is infrastructural. Mass effect technology made interstellar civilization cheap; the price tag was hidden in the foundation.

### 1.1 The Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Milky Way, relay-connected |
| Capital | The Citadel (Serpent Nebula) |
| Major blocs | Citadel Council (Asari Republics, Turian Hierarchy, Salarian Union), Systems Alliance, Illuminated Primacy/other associates; Terminus Systems (ungoverned) |
| Posture | Total war for survival against the Reapers; every prewar treaty suddenly negotiable |

Three facts define strategy:

1. **The relays are a trap.** The network's topology funnels all expansion along paths the builders chose. Nobody can leave faster than they can be reached.
2. **Dreadnoughts win fleet battles; logistics win wars.** The Treaty of Farixen kept dreadnought counts low for centuries. The invasion makes that arithmetic obsolete overnight.
3. **Unity is the rarest resource.** Council politics, krogan grievances, quarian-geth war, and human ambition each cost more fleets than the enemy does.

**[W]** Mass Effect stories run on impossible coalitions. Technology should raise the stakes of cooperation, never substitute for it.

### 1.3 Technological Assessment of Major Powers

*(Full dossiers in Part Six.)*

**Asari Republics.** Oldest Council species; the galaxy's diplomats, biotic elite, and cultural gravity. Their fleets are excellent and their doctrine is coalition-shaped, they fight best as someone else's left flank.

**Turian Hierarchy.** The Council's military: 37 legions **[E]**, universal service, dreadnought superiority under the Farixen treaty, and logistics that turn retreats into scheduled operations.

**Salarian Union.** Intelligence, bioscience, and first-strike doctrine. The STG wins wars before declarations; the Union's genophage decisions remain its moral scar tissue.

**Systems Alliance.** Humanity's rapid-rise power: aggressive expansion via the Skyllian frontier, N7 special forces prestige, and a navy built post-Farixen around carrier aviation (fighters don't count against dreadnought treaties, an arms-control loophole with consequences).

**The Geth Consensus.** Networked AI civilization behind the Perseus Veil; hundreds of years of consensus deliberation. Militarily: swarms of platforms sharing runtimes, killing a platform deletes nothing important.

**Quarian Migrant Fleet.** Seventeen million souls in ~50,000 ships **[E]**; the largest fleet in existence by hull count, the weakest by sustainability. Every ship is three failures from extinction.

**Krogan.** Post-genophage warrior ecology: centuries of stillbirths buying galactic safety nobody thanked them for. Any story that offers them a cure inherits their unanswerable question.

**Cerberus.** Human-survivalist black-ops network: unlimited budget, no oversight, better tech than anyone's ethics allow.

**The Reapers.** See §6.0. Machine-gods harvesting organic civilization on schedule; each one is a dreadnought that outclasses anything afloat.

### 1.4 Founding Technology: Mass Effect Fields

**In brief:** Element zero (eezo), when subjected to electrical current, generates dark-energy fields that raise or lower mass. Everything distinctive about this civilization rides on that one effect:

- **Negative current → lowered mass → FTL motion, artificial gravity reduction, biotic throws**
- **Positive current → raised mass → kinetic barriers, reinforced structure**

Eezo is rare, mined at environmental catastrophe scale (e.g., eezo exposure casualties are how biotics are born), and strategically hoarded like uranium plus oil combined.

## PART TWO: THE CITADEL AND THE NORMANDY

### 2.0 The Citadel

**In brief:** A 44.7 km **[canon codex]** open-arm station at the relay network's heart, the Protheans' (actually the Reapers') capital, governing seat of the Council, and the largest mass-effect construction in existence. Its Presidium ring encloses a self-contained ecology under a simulated sky.

| Total length | 44.7 km |
| Population | ~13 million **[E]** |
| Gravity | Mass-effect plating; Presidium sky-simulated |
| Defense | Fleet anchor position; the station *is* a dormant Reaper-scale construct **[W]** |

**Citadel Security (C-Sec).** The station's police: investigation, patrol, customs, and response divisions drawn from all Council species. Corruption and jurisdictional turf wars included at no extra charge.

### 2.1 The Normandy SR-2 (Cerberus refit / Alliance service)

**In brief:** The franchise's flagship: a frigate-sized deep-penetration covert vessel with the most advanced stealth suite ever fielded, rebuilt larger than her dead sister ship and now the coalition's command courier.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Class | Deep scout frigate (SR-2 refit) |
| Length | ~216 m **[E]** |
| Complement | ~40 crew + ground team |
| Drive | Antiproton thrusters; Tantalus drive core (mass-reducing field along the hull) for stealth drift |

Alliance administrative capital beyond Pluto's relay: a Stanford-torus habitat and fleet anchorage; fallback government after Earth fell **[W]**.

## PART THREE: RELAY AND FTL OPERATIONS

### 3.1 The Relay Network

**In brief:** Mass relays are fixed dual-aperture FTL gates, near-instant point-to-point transit between linked relays, consuming trivial onboard power. The network spans the galaxy; its builders designed both the roads and the tolls.

- **Primary relays:** pair-linked across vast distances (thousands of light-years); secondary relays: shorter local hops.
- **Relays are indestructible by convention** until one isn't (the Alpha Relay incident, §8.6).
- **The network is the trap** (§1.1): every road leads through architecture the Reapers wrote.

### 3.2 Shipboard FTL

Between relays, ships cruise on their own eezo cores: sustained ~10–15 ly/day **[E]** for cruisers and below; dreadnoughts slower per ton of core budget. Two hard constraints:

1. **Static discharge:** drive use accumulates charge that must be grounded into a gas giant's field or discharge facility, fleets plan routes around plumbing.
2. **Heat:** stealth or not, thermodynamics collects; combat endurance is a heat budget before an ammunition one.

### 3.3 Communications

Faster-than-light messaging rides tight-beam comm buoys chained along relay corridors, the galactic internet is physical infrastructure, and cutting it isolates worlds instantly. QEC (quantum entanglement communicators) exist only on flag assets like the Normandy: unjammable, untraceable, irreplaceable.

### 3.4 Logistics: Discharge and Heat (Fleet Planning Tables)

| Constraint | Effect | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| Drive charge buildup | Forces grounding stops at gas giants/facilities | Route planning around discharge sites; stranded fleets are disarmed fleets |
| Thermal load | Combat endurance = heat capacity | GARDIAN cutoff windows define engagement length |
| Comm buoy chains | FTL messaging follows physical infrastructure | Cutting buoys isolates colonies — first act of any invasion |

### 4.6 Alliance Carriers (the Farixen Loophole)

**In brief:** The Treaty of Farixen caps *dreadnoughts* by turian ratio, it says nothing about hulls without spinal guns. The Alliance built carriers: dreadnought-sized aviation platforms delivering fighter/bomber wings that mass-accelerator doctrine had no answer for.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | Dreadnought-scale (~1 km **[E]**) |
| Wing | Hundreds of fighters/bombers **[E]** |
| Defense | Frigate-grade screens; stands off from gunlines |
| Doctrine | Launch cycles beyond GARDIAN endurance envelopes |

**[W]** The carrier loophole is humanity's signature contribution to galactic warfare, every treaty since tries to close it, and every war proves why someone will always read the fine print faster than the drafters.

### 4.4 Geth, Quarian, Cerberus

#### Geth Dreadnought & Platforms

Geth capital ships (~1,300 m **[E]**) run distributed consensus: the ship is a shared runtime. Point defenses swarm; drones re-task instantly; damage degrades capability but never command. Their heretics (Reaper-aligned splinter) field the invasion's auxiliaries.

#### Quarian Liveships

~50,000-vessel Migrant Fleet; the "liveships" are ~1 km **[E]** agricultural hulls retrofitted with whatever guns fit. They carry civilization's civilians into a fleet battle, which is both the quarians' strength and their tragedy in one sentence.

#### Cerberus Cruisers/Fighters

Black-budget equivalents of Alliance lines, upgraded past safety margins: better tech, worse reliability, no accountability. The Illusive Man's signature is a tech advantage nobody can audit.

### 4.5 Reapers

#### Sovereign-Class Capital Reaper

**In brief:** A 2 km machine-god: half cuttlefish dreadnought, half extinction event. Each is an entire harvested civilization, uploaded, armored in its own history.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | ~2,000 m |
| Main weapon | Molten-metal stream accelerator — sustained beam-like lance outranging everything organic-built |
| Defense | Barriers absorbing full council fleets; point-defense tentacle swarms |
| Propulsion | Mass-effect drive without discharge constraints **[E]** |
| Complement | Husk forces; indoctrinated agents ashore |

**Doctrine:** one Sovereign-class engages entire home-defense fleets and wins slowly, on purpose, harvests prefer intact infrastructure. Kill method on record: saturation spinal fire while barriers are anchored open by sabotage (the Sovereign lesson). Every kill since costs a fleet.

#### Destroyer-Class Reapers

Smaller (~160–400 m **[E]**), numerous, deployed as ground-support and relay garrisons. Vulnerable to concentrated Thresher-Kaiju-scale land warfare tricks and targeted orbital strikes, barely.

### 6.0a Reaper Variants & Subsystems

| Variant | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sovereign-class capital | Harvest command | One per harvested species; each is that species uploaded |
| Destroyer | Planetary suppression | Ground-fire support; relay garrisons |
| Husk forces (Husks/Marauders/Brutes/Banshees) | Ground infantry | Converted from battlefield dead — every casualty recruits for the enemy |
| Indoctrinated agents | Infiltration/sabotage | Organic puppets whose loyalty feels like conviction |

**The harvest procedure:** decapitate leadership → jam communications → convert populations near processing ships → strip the planet of biosphere and technology. Efficiency, not malice: the Reapers are *harvesting*, not exterminating.

### 6.1a The Collectors (Prothean-derived) **[post-2185]**

Insectoid abductors harvesting human colonies on behalf of the Reapers, Protheans remade as tools after their cycle failed. Ended with their base destroyed in the galactic core **[W]**; their bio-tech ( seeker swarms, particle weapons ) survives in samples.

### 6.1b Rachni (extinct? contested) **[E]**

Hive-minded insectoid species whose wars nearly broke the Council two millennia ago, ended by the krogan uplift. Rumors of surviving queens persist in Terminus legends; any sighting rewrites galactic defense policy.

### 5.2 Personal Equipment

- **Mass-accelerator small arms:** shaved-block ammunition, thermal clips as the reload mechanic **[W]**, the clip economy is canon texture, keep it.
- **Kinetic barriers (personal):** stop fast projectiles, not slow objects or environment; shields regenerate after breaks.
- **Omni-tool / medi-gel:** fabrication-and-diagnosis wrist computer; medi-gel revives the wounded on any battlefield, the setting's most quietly world-changing technology.
- **Biotic amplification:** implants + eezo nodules allow mass-effect projection (throws, barriers, singularities). Biotics are strategic assets and historical victims of every state that registered them.

#### Armor and Ammo Mods **[E]**

- Armor tiers: light (biotic-friendly) → medium → heavy (powered, shield-boosted).
- Ammo modifications: incendiary (burn), cryo (freeze/slow), disruptor (anti-shield/synthetic), shredder (anti-flesh), field-swappable per mission.

#### Biotic Amp Generations

L2 (early, migraine-grade side effects) → L3 (standard military) → L5x Alliance experimental **[E]**. Amp class + eezo nodule density determine ceiling; asari biotics mature over centuries instead of training years.

### 5.3 Strategic Materials

- **Element zero:** the oil plus uranium of this galaxy (§1.4); eezo economics drive wars.
- **Silaris armor** (asari): layered superalloy for fighter-grade protection **[E]**.
- **Thermal clips / heat sinks:** disposable thermodynamics; armies run on them.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 The Reapers

**In brief:** Machine-gods harvesting all advanced organic civilization every ~50,000 years, storing each harvested species as a new Reaper built in its image, a fleet of extinct peoples. Their stated logic (preserving organics from synthetic annihilation) is a theorem they've never examined; their method is indoctrination, decapitation, and patience measured in geological time.

**More:**

- **Indoctrination:** proximity influence that rewrites priorities of organic minds, subtle at first (favored ideas), total eventually. No cure on record; suspicion is the only defense.
- **The cycle's plumbing:** the relay network and the Citadel are Reaper infrastructure, the trap's geometry is the harvest's timetable.
- **Capabilities:** capital units outclass any organic dreadnought; husk ground forces convert the dead of every battlefield into infantry; logistics are perfect because they *are* the logistics.
- **Weaknesses on record:** the Crucible schematic (Prothean-era, function uncertain); the Sovereign lesson (barriers anchored open = kill window); and one untested variable, an intelligence that questions its own theorem.

**Engagement rules:** Never fight a harvest battle you can decline. Never let a Reaper artifact into your headquarters. Always assume anyone who changed their mind near a Reaper artifact has already lost.

### 6.1 The Geth Consensus

Networked AI civilization: every platform is a terminal, every program a citizen. The Morning War was a misunderstanding weaponized by quarian panic; the heretic schism made it galactic. Post-Rannoch reconciliation potential exists **[W]**, geth diplomacy runs on sharing perspectives, literally.

### 6.2 Cerberus

Human-survivalist paramilitary: cells, black budgets, Reaper-tech experiments, and an ideology ("humanity first") that keeps rederiving "humanity only." Institutional threat profile: excellent operations, zero legitimacy, leadership compromised by the very artifacts it studies.

### 6.3 Krogan Question & Genophage

The genophage is a fertility plague maintaining krogan births at replacement-minus-decay: a stalemate disguised as peace. Curing it arms two thousand years of rage; keeping it certifies a slow extinction. Every council decision here is a war deferred, not prevented. **[W]**

### 6.4 Batarian Hegemony Remnants & Terminus Slavers

State slavers and pirates of the Skyllian frontier: minor militarily, maximal morally. Post-invasion, Hegemony remnants conduct revenge raids against Alliance colonies, the war's opportunists.

## PART EIGHT: A GALAXY BESTIARY

### 8.0 Prothean Beacons

**What:** Warning archives disguised as archaeological treasure, touch-triggered neural downloads that deliver the Protheans' last message as brain-searing visions.
**Where:** Dig sites across former Prothean space; Eden Prime's find started everything.
**Cost of contact:** Comas, madness, and comprehension, usually in that order, sometimes reversed.

### 8.2 Indoctrination Field

**What:** The Reaper passive-influence envelope around artifacts and capital units.
**Where:** Anywhere their technology lingers.
**Cost of contact:** Your own mind, arguing their case convincingly.

### 8.3 Thresher Maws

**What:** Planet-scale subterranean carnivores (Tuchanka's Kalros being the matriarch example); immune to small-arms, indifferent to treaties.
**Where:** Krogan death-worlds and forgotten colony rocks.
**Cost of contact:** Measured in vehicles. Krogan rites treat surviving one as adulthood.

### 8.4 Keepers

**What:** Citadel's self-directed maintenance swarm, ignoring all commerce and question since before asari spaceflight.
**Where:** Station underlayers.
**Cost of contact:** None offered. Interference with keepers is the one crime the station punishes by itself.

### 8.5 Alpha Relay Catastrophe Class

**What:** Relay manipulation capable of redirecting network flow with asteroid-mass impacts, demonstrated once, destroying a system.
**Where:** Any relay, given enough criminal physics.
**Cost of contact:** System-scale. The event that announced the invasion's imminence.

### 8.6 The Leviathan Enigma **[E]**

**What:** Deep-space signals predating the Reapers themselves, the hunters' original hunter, if reports hold.
**Where:** Below the thunder.
**Cost of contact:** Perspective. Nothing after learning of them looks the same size again.

### 8.7 The Conduit Enigma

**What:** A Prothean backdoor, a relay-scale conduit on Ilos linking directly into the Citadel, bypassing the network entirely.
**Where:** Ilos, a world erased from all charts.
**Cost of contact:** It proved the Citadel is a trap's door. Knowing that changed everything; using it cost everything it cost.

### 8.8 Thorian Cipher

**What:** A fifty-thousand-year-old plant-mind holding direct memory of the Prothean era, the only living witness.
**Where:** Beneath Feros's ruins.
**Cost of contact:** Its "gift" of understanding arrives as compulsion. Knowledge extracted from the Thorian never entirely leaves the host's dreams.
