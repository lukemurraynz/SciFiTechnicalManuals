# HALO: TECHNICAL MANUAL

**UNSC NAVAL & GROUND FORCES REFERENCE, FIRST EDITION**

*Sixth entry in the collected set. Prepared in the tradition of the Star Trek Writers'/DS9 Technical Manuals: an in-universe reference consolidating established canon, with clearly marked engineering extrapolation.*

---

## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This manual provides the detailed technical background that stories in the Human–Covenant War era and its aftermath sometimes need. It supplements character work, this setting's drama is endurance against annihilation, and every system here exists to measure that endurance honestly.

**Edition pin.** This First Edition reflects status quo as of **March 2553 CE, the Great War concluded, the Covenant broken, reconstruction under way** (see §1.5).

**Conventions used throughout:**

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(no marker)* | Established by game/lore canon |
| **[E]** | Extrapolation where canon was silent |
| **[W]** | Writer's latitude note |

Canon wins over this manual; log conflicts in Appendix C.

## PART ONE: THE THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

For twenty-seven years humanity lost every fleet engagement it fought until it learned to win by any means available, including the ones nobody signs off on. This manual documents both the war machine and what the war made of it.

**In brief:** UNSC technology is inferior, mass-produced, and crewed by the last species in the galaxy still willing to die for a colony.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Orion Arm, Sol and Outer Colony regions |
| Power blocs | United Nations Space Command (UNSC), shattered Covenant remnants, Banished mercenary federation **[E/post-war]**, insurrectionist holdouts |
| Existential hazards | Flood quarantine zones, Forerunner installation security systems |
| Posture | Reconstruction; ONI information control at wartime levels |

Defining facts:

1. **The Cole Protocol saved the species:** no ship may lead the enemy to Earth. Navigation data was burned for twenty-seven years.
2. **Slipspace is common ground:** both sides travel the same slipstream sea; jumps leave wakes readable for days.
3. **The Covenant broke, not surrendered.** Species that fought together for decades now fight each other, and some of them blame humans personally.

### 1.2 Consolidated Timeline

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| ~100,000 years ago | Forerunner–Flood war ends with Halo installations firing; galaxy reseeded. Forerunners depart/ascend, leaving guardians |
| 2160s–2500s | Interplanetary Insurrection era; UNSC rises to dominance; SPARTAN programs conceived |
| 2525 | First Contact: Covenant glassing of Harvest. War begins |
| 2525–2552 | Losing war: Outer Colonies fall one by one; SPARTAN-II program turns the tide tactically, never strategically |
| 2552 | Fall of Reach; Pillar of Autumn finds Halo (Installation 04) — first Halo discovery, detonated rather than fired. Earth invasion repelled at terrible cost |
| 2552–2553 | Schism (Elites vs. Prophets), Ark campaign, Master Chief activates Installation 08; High Prophet of Truth dead; war ends |
| 2553 | **Edition pin:** armistice chaos, remnant hunting, reconstruction |

### 1.3 Assessment of Powers

**UNSC.** Humanity's military government-in-all-but-name: Navy built around MAC doctrine, Marines who never stopped retreating forward, ONI deciding what truth is. Technology human-scale, industrial, and desperate.

**Covenant Remnants.** Theocratic coalition shattered along species lines, Sangheili (Elites) fleets under new kaidons, Jiralhanae (Brutes) packs under Tartarus's successors, San'Shyuum (Prophets) nearly extinct after High Charity's fall. Their technology remains superior per hull; their unity is gone.

**The Banished **[E/post-war]**.** Brute-led mercenary federation harvesting war materiel from everyone's graveyards. Discipline where the Covenant had dogma.

**Flood.** Quarantined, not defeated. See §6.2.

**Forerunner Legacy.** Shield worlds, Guardians, composers, and install-security systems still executing million-year-old directives. Humanity is flagged as "Reclaimer" in their systems, inheritance without instructions.
### 1.4 Founding Technologies

**In brief:** Humanity's war machine rests on three pillars: the Shaw-Fujikawa slipspace drive (FTL by brute force), the Magnetic Accelerator Cannon (fleet combat by mass driver), and the MJOLNIR/SPARTAN synthesis (infantry as strategic asset). Each was a crash program; none would exist without the war that demanded it.

### 1.5 Status Quo Snapshot (Edition Pin: March 2553)

| Domain | Status |
|---|---|
| UNSC Navy | ~30% of prewar tonnage remaining **[E]**; Infinity commissioned as flagship |
| Colonies | Inner colonies battered but holding; Outer Colonies glassed or quarantined |
| Covenant | Dissolved; Sangheili–Jiralhanae war raging on former Covenant worlds |
| ONI | Running the information war: Spartan programs, artifact recovery, truth management |
| Flood | All known outbreak sites sterilized; quarantine doctrine permanent |
| Spartans | SPARTAN-IV branch stood up — volunteers now, legends then |

**[W]** Post-war is a demobilization story: what does an army built to stop extinction do on Tuesday? Every remnant faction exists because that question has no good answer yet.

## PART TWO: UNCS INFINITY (FLAGSHIP)

### 2.0 Overview

**In brief:** The largest warship humanity ever built: a 5.7 km supercarrier-frigate hybrid, secretly constructed through the war's worst years, fitted with Forerunner-derived systems recovered from Onyx, and crewed by the best of everyone.

| Property | Value (production-verified) |
|---|---|
| Class | Infinity-class supercapital |
| Length × width × height | 5,694.2 m × 833.3 m × 1,041.2 m |
| Complement | ~17,000 total: 5,400 Marines · 750 ODSTs · 1,700 ONI agents · 480 UEG civilian specialists · one full Spartan-IV fire brigade **[E]** · 8 Huragok (recovered engineers) |
| Drive | Slipspace drive with Forerunner-derived accuracy — pinpoint jumps, no realspace recalibration drift **[E]** |
| Armament | 4 × Super-MAC (Series-8) spinal guns; missile batteries in thousands; point-defense nuclear missiles **[E]** |
| Bays | Ten Strident-class frigates internal dock; F-41 Broadsword wings |

**Doctrine:** Infinity is a fleet by herself, carrier, battleship, and expedition base. Her existence is ONI's argument that humanity never again fights a war at material disadvantage.

### 2.1 Command Systems

Bridge redundancy tripled post-war; combat information center runs ship-wide AI support (shipboard AIs remain smart-AIs on seven-year rampancy clocks, see §8.5). Spartan detachments answer directly to the commanding officer, bypassing naval lines, a friction point by design.

### 2.2 Power & Defense

Fusion plants plus salvaged Forerunner capacitors **[E]**; shields are the first human hull-mounted energy barrier suite (reverse-engineered from Covenant capture). Armor: 4.9 m titanium-A3 battleplate over energy-dispersing lattice **[E]**.

## PART THREE: SLIPSPACE OPERATIONS

**In brief:** FTL travel runs through slipspace, a parallel dimension where distance compresses and physics negotiates. Transit requires Shaw-Fujikawa drives punching into slipstream via fusion-timed ruptures.

- **Accuracy scales with drive class:** wartime UNSC jumps arrived within a million kilometers if lucky; Covenant arrived on top of you; post-war Infinity arrives where aimed **[E]**.
- **Wakes:** every jump leaves slipspace scarring readable for days, pursuit and evasion both live in that residue.
- **Communication:** real-time comms exist only within system; interstellar messages ride ships or relay buoys. The war was fought by fleets who learned news weeks late.
- **Hazards:** slipspace anomalies, transit-time variance (subjective vs. real), and things ONI has classified rather than confirmed **[W]**.

Forerunner fortresses/cruisers encountered since operate on ancient security logic, Reclaimer-flagged humans get doors others don't, and weapons nobody wants pointed anywhere.

### 5.1 Naval Weapons

| System | Principle | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Magnetic Accelerator Cannon (MAC) | Electromagnetic slug launch | Spinal mount; one shot per charge cycle; Super-MAC orbital variants crack carriers |
| Archer missiles | Massed conventional ordnance | Thousands per engagement; saturation doctrine |

| Plasma torpedoes (Covenant) | Guided magnetically-contained plasma | Homing, shield-piercing over time; dodgeable by maneuver |

### 5.2 Ground Weapons and Kit

#### UNSC Small-Arms Family

| Weapon | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|

#### Vehicle Roster **[E]**

M12 Warthog (utility/recon/Troop/Gauss variants), M808 Scorpion MBT, M290 Mongoose ATV, UH-144 Falcon tiltrotor, D77 Pelican dropship, B-65 Shortsword bomber.

**Armor:** MJOLNIR powered assault armor, full-body exoskeleton with energy shielding and neural uplink; *only Spartan-augmented bodies survive wearing it* (unaugmented volunteers become statistics).

### 5.3 The SPARTAN Programs

**In brief:** Three generations of super-soldier:

| Generation | Selection | Augmentation | Armor |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPARTAN-II | Children screened by genetics/psychology (Halsey protocol) | Full skeletal/muscular/neural augmentation — high mortality | MJOLNIR Mk IV–VI |
| SPARTAN-III | Orphans of glassed colonies; suicide-battalion economics | Cheaper, riskier augmentations | SPI camouflage / MJOLNIR issue |
| SPARTAN-IV | Adult volunteers, post-war | Safe augmentation suite | MJOLNIR [GEN2] printed **[E]** |

**[W]** Spartans are the war's moral question in armor: what was done to children to save everyone. Every story touching them inherits it.

### 5.4 Strategic Materials

- **Titanium-A battleplate:** standard hull armor; measured in meters on capital hulls.
- **Covenant nanolaminate:** Covenant hull material, stronger, self-assembling; salvage priority one post-war.
- **Forerunner hardlight/alloys:** beyond current manufacture; recovered examples reshape whole programs.

### 5.5 ONI: The Intelligence Arm

Office of Naval Intelligence runs Section Zero (internal affairs), Three (special programs, including Spartans), and everything it doesn't admit to. ONI's wartime truth-management (colonies never told the war was lost) remains its defining moral ledger **[W]**.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 Covenant Remnants

Post-Schism factions fight over the Covenant's corpse: Sangheili kaidon fleets honor-war each other while hunting Prophets' survivors; Jiralhanae packs (and the Banished) harvest everything loose. Their technology still outclasses UNSC per-hull, remnant ships win duels; they no longer win wars of position.

**Engagement rules:** Never assume remnant = weak. Never treat an Elite's honor code as weakness, it is a targeting system. Always ask which Prophet someone still secretly serves.

#### Covenant Species Breakdown (order of battle reference)

| Species | Battlefield role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sangheili (Elites) | Warriors/shipmasters | Honor culture; post-schism faction leaders |
| Jiralhanae (Brutes) | Shock troops | Pack hierarchy; Covenant's post-Schism core |
| Kig-Yar (Jackals/Skirmishers) | Skirmishers/marksmen | Mercenary instinct; energy gauntlets |
| Unggoy (Grunts) | Swarm infantry | Methane breathers; morale breaks without oversight |
| Mgalekgolo (Hunters) | Anti-armor pairs | Colonies of eel-worms in armor; immune to most small arms |
| Yanme'e (Drones) | Flying swarms | Mechanical-minded insectoids |
| San'Shyuum (Prophets) | Leadership | Nearly extinct post-High Charity |
| Huragok (Engineers) | Maintenance | Neutral, gentle, priceless — they fix anything **[W]** |

### 6.1 The Banished **[E/post-war]**

Brute-founded mercenary federation: captured Covenant manufacturing, disciplined pack doctrine, zero theology. They fight for assets, not gods, which makes them predictable economically and unstoppable operationally.

### 6.2 The Flood

**In brief:** Parasitic superspecies: infection converts any nervous tissue into biomass serving a galaxy-mind (Gravemind). The Forerunners built the Halo array specifically to starve it.

- **Stages:** infection forms → combat forms from corpses → pure forms → Gravemind (when biomass crosses critical mass, the mind wakes and speaks in every voice it has eaten).
- **Strategy:** the Flood doesn't hate; it incorporates. Spaceflight-capable victims spread it between systems.
- **Counter:** total sterilization. Quarantine doctrine exists because "total" failed once per century somewhere **[W]**.
- **The Halo option:** firing installations starves Flood by killing its food, all sentient life. The weapon is the dilemma.

### 6.3 Promethean Constructs

Forerunner war-machines (Knights, Watchers, Crawlers), composed of digitized ancient minds executing security directives. Hard-light weaponry, teleportation, and target discrimination that flags humanity oddly ("Reclaimer" privileges cut both ways). Post-war encounters concentrate around Shield worlds and Guardians waking across colonized space **[W]**.

## PART EIGHT: A WAR BESTIARY

### 8.0 The Halo Installations

**What:** Seven (originally twelve) ringworlds, 10,000 km diameter, built by the Forerunners as research stations and galactic-scale weapons, firing together, they sterilize all sentient life within three radii of galactic center, starving the Flood.
**Where:** Distributed across the Orion Arm; Installation 04 destroyed, 05 damaged, 08 fired once at low power.
**Cost of contact:** Each ring is a loaded species-wide gun with a library attached. The libraries say the gun was mercy.

### 8.2 The Composer

**What:** Forerunner device converting minds into digital essence, immortality technology used as a weapon (New Phoenix incident, 2557-era operations).
**Where:** Seized/recovered under ONI seal **[W]**
**Cost of contact:** Everyone it takes is still in there.

### 8.3 Shield Worlds / Requiem-class

**What:** Dyson-shell artificial planets housing ecumene garrisons in stasis.
**Where:** Onyx system (destroyed), Requiem (sun-crashed), others suspected.
**Cost of contact:** Opening one wakes whatever was locked inside, by design or by failure.

### 8.4 Rampancy

**What:** Smart AI death sentence: after ~7 years of operation, knowledge density collapses into delusion, megalomania, melancholy, or obsession, then death. Cortana's case rewrote the rulebook **[W/post-pin]**.
**Where:** Every shipboard smart AI's calendar.
**Cost of contact:** Your most trusted officer, dying in slow motion while running your ship.

### 8.5 Slipspace Anomalies

**What:** Wakes, rifts, and something ONI calls "traffic" that isn't anyone's **[W]**.
**Where:** Deep slipstream lanes.
**Cost of contact:** Unrecorded. That's why it's classified.
