## ABOUT THIS MANUAL

This manual orients new personnel aboard Talos I, TranStar Industries' premier orbital research station: home of the Neuromod program, the Typhon research initiative, and humanity's most valuable patent portfolio.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **the station-wide emergency declared during the annual shareholder demonstration (2032 CE)**. Subsequent events are under review.

---

## PART ONE: ORBITAL THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

Talos I orbits the Moon where a Cold War satellite defense platform once watched for Soviet missiles. TranStar rebuilt it around something stranger: an alien structure recovered from deep space, older than human civilization, which we did not build and cannot fully enter. Everything valuable aboard grew from studying it.

**In brief:** The station is a laboratory built around its own experiment, staffed by people who forgot the experiment predates them.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Lunar orbit |
| Complement | ~500 personnel: scientists, support staff, security **[E]** |
| Assets | Neuromod patents, Typhon specimens, psychotronics research |
| Contingency | Self-destruct protocol requires CEO authorization and reactor override |

Defining facts:

1. **Neuromods are the product.** Instant skill acquisition from injectable Typhon-derived material: surgery plus memory integration. One neuromod makes you a marksman, a hacker, a surgeon.
2. **Typhon research outranks safety culture** when quarterly results are due **[W]**.
3. **Earth is one infected shuttle away.** Station containment failures are planetary threats by definition.

**Station orbit:** Earth-Moon L2 Lagrange point. **Sector codes:** LOB000 lobby, LOB01 shuttle bay, LOB02 neuromod division, LOB03 psychotronics (built around the original Kletka containment unit), LOB04 hardware labs, LIF000/001 life support and cargo **[canon]**.

### 1.3 Assessment of Forces

**TranStar Industries.** Earth-side corporate parent: quarterly-report ethics, unlimited research ambition.

**Station Security.** Operators with non-lethal disruptors by policy (liability), lethal options in armories by precedent.

**The Typhon.** See Part Six. Black shadow-matter organisms that read as "living physics experiments": they obey rules we have only begun to chart.

**Looking Glass Group / January Protocol.** A hidden contingency: recordings and a duplicate AI guiding selected personnel through outbreak response. Its existence implies leadership expected this **[W]**.

## PART THREE: TRANSPORT AND COMMUNICATIONS

Shuttle transfers to Earth run on schedule until quarantines. Internal transit: tram spine connecting sections plus maintenance crawlspaces. Communications: station intercom, personal transcribes, and Earth uplink via corporate relay. During the outbreak, every channel carries either propaganda, panic, or both.

## PART FOUR: RESEARCH ASSETS

### 4.1 Neuromods

**In brief:** Injectable skill packages: each neuromod wipes the user's recent memory (a documented side effect management prefers understated) and installs complete expertise: hacking, leverage, mimicking matter, firing weapons you never trained on.

- Fabricated from Typhon biomass at Psychotronics.
- Side effects include Typhon empathy drift at high usage counts **[W]**.

## PART FIVE: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 5.0 The Typhon

**In brief:** Shadow-black organisms of alien origin, apparently older than human civilization, operating as a coordinated ecology rather than individual monsters. They learn from us: every countermeasure we develop, their next generation has already seen.

| Organism | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Mimic** | Disguises as everyday objects; swarms prey when approached. The station's signature horror |
| **Phantom** | Human-derived tall figures; elemental variants (thermal/electrical/pure); retain fragments of the person copied |
| **Technopath** | Hijacks Operators, turrets, and systems remotely |
| **Telepath** | Mind-control broadcast; compels humans to attack; vulnerable in dialogue before violence |
| **Nightmare** | Station-hunting apex form deployed against high-Typhon-aptitude personnel |

**Kill doctrine:** conventional fire works but wastes resources; Typhon die to disruption (Disruptor Stun Gun), fire, and especially to *their own* recycled neuromod material turned into weapons **[E]**.

### 5.1 Indoctrination Analogue: The Coral

**What:** Post-outbreak, Typhon growth ("the Coral") networks across the station exterior like neural tissue wrapped around the hull. It reads as a distributed brain forming around the station's bones.
**Cost of contact:** Touching it connects minds. Some connections do not fully close.

## PART SEVEN: HAZARD BESTIARY

### 7.0 Mimic Ambush Doctrine

**What:** The signature kill: an object you passed ten times was a Mimic for the last three.
**Doctrine:** Psychoscope sweeps, chair-counting discipline, and paranoia as a professional skill.

### 7.2 The Coral Cascade

**What:** Networked Typhon growth linking station systems to alien processing. Destroying the Coral threatens the station's structural integrity; preserving it feeds something larger.
**Cost of contact:** Choices about the Coral are choices about what humanity becomes next door to Earth.

### 7.3 Self-Destruct Contingency

**What:** Reactor overload scuttles Talos I into lunar surface rather than risk Earth contamination. Authorization: CEO biometric + reactor override.
**Cost of contact:** One lever ends the patent portfolio and the plague together.
