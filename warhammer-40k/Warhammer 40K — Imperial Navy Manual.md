## ABOUT THIS SANCTIONED TEXT

This text instructs officers of the Imperial Navy in the conduct of void war across the God-Emperor's dominion. Knowledge is a weapon; ignorance is heresy; this volume is therefore loaded.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **M41.999, the era of the Great Rift, the Thirteenth Black Crusade broken at Cadia's grave** (see §1.5).

## PART ONE: THE THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

There is only war. The Imperium of Man spans a million worlds held by faith, fleet, and the corpses of both. The Navy is the rope binding a million scattered candles against a night with teeth.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | The Milky Way — Segmentae Solar, Obscurus, Ultima, Tempestus, Pacificus |
| Threats | Chaos (the Archenemy), Ork WAAAGH!s, Tyranid hive fleets, Necron dynasties, T'au expansion, Aeldari machinations, heresy within |
| Posture | Total war everywhere, forever; victory defined as continued existence |

Defining facts:

1. **The Astronomican lights the way**, the Emperor's psychic beacon, ten thousand years burning, guiding Navigators through the Warp.
2. **The Warp is both road and predator.** All FTL rides its tides; every jump risks soul-consumption.
3. **The Imperium cannot lose quickly or win slowly.** Attrition is strategy; martyrdom is logistics.

### 1.2 Consolidated Timeline

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| M25–M29 | Dark Age of Technology; humanity's machine golden age ends in catastrophe |
| M30 | The Emperor's Great Crusade reunifies the galaxy; the Horus Heresy betrays it at its zenith |
| M31–M40 | Ten millennia of siege-state: the Imperium ossifies into faith and attrition |
| M41.999 | Great Rift tears the galaxy; Cadia falls; the Thirteenth Black Crusade breaks. **Pin** |

### 1.3 Threat Powers (Summary)

Chaos corrupts, Orks swarm, Tyranids devour, Necrons wake, Aeldari scheme, five apocalypses queued, each certain it is first in line. Full dossiers: Part Six.

### 1.4 Founding Technologies

**In brief:** Three miracles hold the Imperium together: Navigators reading the Astronomican through the Warp's madness, Gellar fields keeping souls aboard ships that jump, and void shields/lance batteries making cathedrals seaworthy in hell. All are maintained by a priesthood that worships what it cannot reproduce.

### 1.5 Status Quo Snapshot (M41.999)

| Domain | Status |
|---|---|
| The Rift | Cicatrix Maledictum torn across the galaxy; Warp bleeds into realspace |
| Cadia | Destroyed ("Cadia stands before it fell") |
| Fleet posture | Battlefleet mobilization galaxy-wide; reinforcements arrive decades late or centuries early |
| Communication | Astropathic choir messages — psychic, allegorical, unreliable |

## PART TWO: BATTLEFLEET OPERATIONS

### 2.0 The Imperial Ship

**In brief:** Imperial capital vessels are cathedral-fortresses: kilometers of gothic armor, plasma drives the size of towns, crews in the tens of thousands, and a lifespan measured in millennia of service and retrofit.

### 2.2 Void Combat Doctrine

- **Broadside geometry:** ships fight broadside-on, presenting maximum guns and minimum profile, Age of Sail with plasma.
- **Lances vs. batteries:** lances burn through void shields point-first; macro shells saturate.
- **Void shields:** layered warp-membranes that collapse under saturation and recharge after, timing windows decide duels.
- **Boarding actions:** holy and common; armsmen hold corridors while tech-adeptes defend the enginarium.

### 2.3 Cruiser Departments (command orientation)

| Department | Function | Personnel |
|---|---|---|
| The Bridge / command spire | Lord-Captain's authority, Navigator's sanctum adjoining | Officers, servitor banks |
| Gunnery decks | Macro-battery crews by the thousand; shell lifts from magazines | Pressed crews, gun-marshals |
| Lance citadels | Beam capacitor banks and focusing shrines | Tech-adeptes |
| Enginarium | Plasma drives, reactor shrines | Mechanicus priesthood only **[W]** |
| Life decks | Crew habitation — hereditary ship families evolve within a generation | Population of a town, born aboard |
| Launch bays | Fury interceptors, Starhawk bombers | Wing commanders, flight chaplains |

**Crew culture:** ships are dynasties. Families live and die in the same corridors for centuries; mutiny is heresy, and heresy is contagious, hence the Commissariat.

## PART THREE: THE WARP AND NAVIGATION

**In brief:** All FTL transits the Warp, a psychic dimension where emotion is weather and predators are native. Transit requires:

1. **Navigators**, mutated human bloodlines whose third eye perceives the Astronomican.
2. **Gellar fields**, reality membranes keeping the ship (and souls) intact inside the sea of souls.
3. **Astropathic choirs**, psychic messaging; allegorical, delayed, occasionally screaming.

**Hazards:** warp storms (regions severed for centuries), time dilation (arriving before departure happens), and daemonic incursion when Gellars fail. Every jump is a prayer with mathematics attached.

## PART FIVE: WEAPONS AND SANCTIONED ARMAMENT

- **Macro-cannons:** massed shell broadsides; reload crews by the thousand.
- **Lance batteries:** sustained beam cuts through shields and armor alike.
- **Nova cannon:** spinal mass driver firing at extreme range, misses are still area events.
- **Torpedoes:** crewed or uncrewed; some carry plasma, some carry worse.
- **Exterminatus:** cyclonic torpedoes/virus bombardment rendering worlds lifeless, authorization: Inquisition/Lord High Admiral tier. The Navy's final argument.

### 5.6 Ground Forces (transport responsibility)

The Astra Militarum (Guard): billions of regiments; the Navy delivers them into the fire on schedule. Astartes: eight-foot transhuman monks in ceramite, transport them respectfully.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 Chaos and the Archenemy

**In brief:** The Warp's gods (Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle, Slaanesh) cultivate mortal corruption: traitor legions (the Horus Heresy's heirs, ten millennia traitor), daemon engines, cults within every hive world. The Black Crusades test the Cadian Gate every generation.

**Engagement rules:** Purity of faith is armor. Never board a derelict without a priest. Never read what the enemy writes.

#### The Four Great Enemies of the Soul

| Power | Domain | Temptation |
|---|---|---|
| Khorne | Blood, war, rage | Violence offered as strength |
| Tzeentch | Change, scheming, hope | Knowledge and "just one more plan" |
| Nurgle | Decay, despair, endurance through rot | Relief from pain and fear of death |
| Slaanesh | Excess, sensation, perfection | Everything beautiful taken one step too far |

Each god fields traitor legions, daemon engines, and cults. The Traitor Legions of the Horus Heresy remain their champions: ten millennia traitor, still fighting the battle they lost at Terra's gates.

### 6.2 The Tyranid Hive Fleets

Extragalactic devourers: bio-fleets consuming biospheres, adapting to every wound. Each fleet destroyed reveals three more inbound beyond the galactic rim. Genestealer cults precede them by generations, infiltration as appetite.

### 6.3 Orks

Fungal war-race: spore-reproducing, technology that works because they believe it does, WAAAGH!s that turn aggression into physics. Endless, cheerful, unstoppable except by greater enthusiasm plus superior firepower.

### 6.4 Necrons & Aeldari

Elder races waking (Necron dynasties) and dying (Aeldari craftworlds) around the Imperium's edges; both possess technology the Mechanicus covets and cannot replicate. Treat contact as archaeology with lasers.

- **Faith is force multiplication:** liturgy precedes launch; morale is doctrine.

## PART EIGHT: A GRIM BESTIARY

### 8.0 The Warp

**What:** The sea of souls underlying reality, realm of daemons, mirror of emotion, highway of empire.
**Where:** Beneath everything.
**Cost of contact:** Your soul, eventually. Gellar fields are the only umbrella.

### 8.2 The Astronomican

**What:** The Emperor's psychic lighthouse, powered by a thousand psykers' daily sacrifice.
**Where:** Terra, visible to Navigators across the galaxy.
**Cost of contact:** It burns a thousand souls a day so a million worlds can find their way home. The arithmetic of the Imperium in one sentence.

### 8.3 Exterminatus

**What:** World-killing sanction: cyclonic torpedoes or virus bombardment erasing biospheres to deny them to the enemy.
**Where:** Anywhere heresy wins.
**Cost of contact:** A world. The Inquisition keeps count; the count has no bottom.

### 8.4 Genestealer Cults

**What:** Tyranid vanguard infiltrations, four-armed prophets building insurrections generations before the fleet arrives.
**Where:** Undercity altars on every productive world.
**Cost of contact:** The uprising is the appetizer.

### 8.5 The Golden Throne

**What:** The Emperor's life-support and psychic anchor: a device consuming a thousand psykers daily to keep the master of mankind half-alive, holding shut the door his greatest son broke.
**Where:** The Imperial Palace, Terra, inner sanctum.
**Cost of contact:** Pilgrims die by the thousand for a glimpse of the outer gate. The Throne is failing; the Mechanicus patches it; nobody says what happens when the patching stops. **[W]**

### 8.6 The Black Library **[W]**

**What:** The Aeldari harlequins' hidden archive of everything Chaos is and was, the only complete map of the enemy, guarded so absolutely that its location is itself a myth.
**Where:** The Webway.
**Cost of contact:** The Imperium denies it exists. The Inquisition's oldest files disagree with the Imperium.
