**YELLOWSTONE EXPANSE SURVIVAL BULLETIN**

*Twenty-third entry in the collected set; house-style variant: authored as an Ultranaut survival bulletin. Hard-science voice throughout; Reynolds' universe punishes miracles.*

---

## ABOUT THIS BULLETIN

This reference documents the Yellowstone Expanse, Conjoiner drive travel, and the Inhibitor crisis as understood by lighthugger crews. It is written for people who already know the void is trying to kill them.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **the Resurgem crisis period (c. 2526 CE)**: the *Nostalgia for Infinity* arrived over Yellowstone, the Melding Plague has gutted Chasm City and the Glitter Band, and something old has woken in the dark between stars.

---

## PART ONE — THE YELLOWSTONE EXPANSE

### 1.0 Introduction

Humanity reached the stars in slow lighthuggers crewed by Ultranauts, settled dozens of systems, and built paradise around Yellowstone: the Glitter Band, thousands of orbitals running direct-democracy by neural implant. Then a passenger from Resurgem brought back more than memories, and the Plague ate paradise from inside. Now the sky belongs to ruins, and something in deep space has noticed us.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |

| Polities | Remnant Demarchist authority, Panoply law enforcement **[E]**, Ultranaut fleet remnants, Shrouder territories |

Defining facts:

1. **The Plague eats both flesh and machine**, corrupting nanotech into grotesque hybrid growths. Chasm City's towers are its sculpture garden.
2. **Conjoiner drives work; nobody knows why anymore.** The Conjoiners build them and stop answering questions.
3. **The Inhibitors have been awake for millions of years,** pruning starfaring species on schedule. Recent events tripped their threshold.

## PART TWO — LIGHTHUGGER OPERATIONS

### 2.0 The Lighthugger

**In brief:** Kilometer-scale Conjoiner-drive starships: black, windowless, faster than anything organic-built has a right to be. Crewed by Ultranauts modified to endure decades of acceleration and vacuum-adjacent life. The *Nostalgia for Infinity* is the type's haunted flagship.

| Property | Value **[E]** |
|---|---|
| Length | 1,500–2,000 m class |
| Hull | Black ablative sheathing; no windows by design (radiation and sanity both) |
| Drive | Conjoiner engine: runs on exotic physics, sealed by the Conjoiners themselves |
| Complement | Dozens of Ultranauts; cryogenic passenger berths for colonists |
| Transit | Interstellar crossings measured in years to decades |

**Ultranaut modifications:** pressure-tolerant physiology, neural interface grafts, pain management cultures. Ultras age slowly, heal oddly, and consider baseline humans fragile cousins.

### 2.1 The Conjoiner Drive Cage

Each lighthugger's drive is a sealed Conjoiner enclave: Exultants living inside the engine, thinking it into operation. Opening the cage kills the ship's soul and invites what the Conjoiners fled. This is not superstition; two ships proved it **[W]**.

## PART THREE — COMMUNICATIONS AND TIME

No FTL communication exists: messages ride ships. Yellowstone-to-Resurgem news arrives years stale. Consequence: every polity governs itself in isolation, every war ends before orders arrive, and every reunion happens between strangers.

## PART FIVE — WEAPONS AND MATERIALS

- **Cache weapons:** Hell-class superweapons recovered from buried caches; they have their own agendas and require negotiation with their internal intelligences before firing **[W]**.
- **Green beam:** Inhibitor signature weapon observed at Resurgem: precision orbital strike from interstellar range.
- **Warp-capable? No.** Nothing here cheats light except the drives, and the drives answer to Conjoiners.

## PART SIX — THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 The Inhibitors

**In brief:** A machine network a billion years old, seeded to suppress starfaring intelligence until some ancient galactic crisis passes. They wake when civilizations cross a threshold, then sterilize them with patient, industrial horror: wolf-shaped hunters, neutron-star-forged machinery, and time on their side.

**Engagement rules:** You do not fight the Inhibitors. You hide, you run, or you become part of their evidence.

### 6.1 The Melding Plague

Nanotechnological plague that rewrites both machine and flesh into baroque hybrid growths. It cannot be cured, only survived: abandon infected tech, amputate infected tissue, and never trust a "cure." Chasm City is its masterpiece.

### 6.2 The Shrouders

Stealth-wrapped alien species whose territories rewrite physics as defense. Probing them bought humanity exactly one useful technology and a permanent warning label.
