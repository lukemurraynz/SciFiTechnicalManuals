## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This manual documents the species, ships, and hazards of the Uncharted Territories, the lawless border region beyond Peacekeeper command where a lost human astronaut survived by being useful, dangerous, or both.

**Edition pin.** This First Edition reflects status quo as of **early in the crew's third cycle together: post-Scarran contact, pre- wormhole-weapons crisis** (see §1.5).

## PART ONE: THE TERRITORIES

### 1.0 Introduction

The Uncharted Territories sit between the Peacekeeper armada's discipline and every power too strange or stubborn to be conquered. Ships are alive, weapons talk, and the local equivalent of a passport is a reputation for being more trouble than you're worth.

**In brief:** No maps hold, no laws bind, and the fastest ship in the region is a pregnant leviathan with a criminal crew.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Uncharted Territories + neighboring power spheres |
| Powers | Peacekeepers (militarized mercenaries), Scarran Imperium (heat-based empire), Nebari establishment, Hynerian dominion, Diagnosans, Luxan clans |
| Posture | Cold war between Peacekeepers and Scarrans, fought through proxies and prisoners |

Defining facts:

1. **Peacekeepers enforce order as a product**, protection, discipline, and genetic breeding programs instead of a homeland.
2. **Scarrans run on heat:** their caste power literally radiates; their empire annexes by converting subject biology.
3. **Wormholes are the ultimate weapon:** stable wormhole knowledge would make any holder unbeatable, everyone is looking; one human accidentally knows.

### 1.2 Consolidated Timeline

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| Ancient era | Eidolons build peace-cultivation civilization; leviathans bred by Builders as living ships |
| Recent centuries | Peacekeeper expansion; Scarren-Shebok? consolidation into Imperium; territories lawless between |
| Cycle 0 (arrival) | Human test pilot's module collides with Peacekeeper vessel during a wormhole experiment; debris kills an officer; fugitive crew forms aboard Moya during her escape |
| Cycles 1–3 | Prison breaks, bounty hunters, neural-clone implant, Crais's defection, Talyn's birth, Scarran first contact. **Pin** |

### 1.3 Assessment of Powers

**Peacekeepers.** Sebacean mercenary armada: carrier command (command carriers ~massive capital ships), strict hierarchy, "reversing the collar" discipline culture. They do not conquer; they *contract*, and punish default with orbital annihilation.

**Scarran Imperium.** Heat-absorbing reptilian empire: stronger individually than Sebaceans, slower politically, crueler than both combined. Strelitzia-class dreadnoughts **[E]** project their will.

**Nebari Establishment.** Mind-control conformity state spreading quietly, the slowest, most complete conquest in the Territories.

**Diagnosans.** The galaxy's greatest surgeons: perfect healers who consider all other life biologically offensive; they wear gloves to touch you.
### 1.4 Founding Technologies

**In brief:** Three systems define life in the Territories: translator microbes (language as colonization), leviathan bio-ships (transport as servitude), and pulse-cell energy weapons (violence as logistics). A fourth, wormhole theory, exists in exactly one head, and that head is the story.

## PART TWO: MOYA (LEVIATHAN BIOMECHANOID)

### 2.0 Overview

**In brief:** A leviathan: a living ship grown by the Builders, part whale, part starship, fully sentient. Leviathans are peaceful by design; their only defense is speed (Starburst) and the pilots bonded to them. Moya broke her Peacekeeper control collar and ran.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Class | Leviathan biomechanoid |
| Length | ~1,000 m? **[E]** (organic, variable) |
| Complement | Crew + Pilot (bonded navigator organism) + DRDs (maintenance drones) |
| Drive | Hetch drive (sublight); Starburst (FTL fold-jump) |
| Offense | None — leviathans are unarmed by Builder design **[W]** |
| Special | Living systems: self-healing hull, organic interiors, empathic bond with Pilot |

```
   LEVIATHAN — PROFILE (schematic)

      ____ pilot den / amnexus ____
     /                             \

   (                                   )
```

**Starburst:** the signature FTL maneuver, hull membranes charge, then the ship folds space in an explosive jump. Direction is imprecise; escape, not navigation.

**Pilot bond:** Pilot and leviathan share nervous system and lifespan; separating either is torture. Pilots serve 200+ cycles and remember everything.

### 2.01 Moya Interior Zones

| Zone | Function |
|---|---|
| Pilot's den / amnexus | Bonded Pilot's chamber — the ship's brain-stem interface; entry is privilege and trespass at once |
| Terraced crew quarters | Organic chambers grown for occupants; doors dilate on the ship's whim |
| Cargo holds | Fugitives, trade goods, Peacekeeper salvage |
| Center chamber | Comm-relay nexus and crew commons |
| Maintenance bays | DRD swarms; anything broken gets licked back to health |
| Neural cluster | The ship's "brain" — damage here is brain damage **[W]** |

**[W]** Aboard a leviathan, the ship is a character: she overheats in anger, grieves, hides. Write Moya scenes as scenes with someone who can't leave the conversation.

### 2.2 Aboard Systems

- **DRDs:** spider-like repair drones maintaining/repairing the living hull; they respond to the ship's moods.
- **Organic architecture:** rooms grow; doors dilate; injuries heal over cycles. Ship damage = bodily harm.
- **Translator microbes:** colonizing bacteria granting instant language comprehension, how a human speaks everything. Universal to the Territories.

## PART THREE: TRAVEL OPERATIONS

**Hetch drive:** sublight cruising between systems. **Starburst:** escape FTL, imprecise. **Wormholes:** rare natural tunnels across the universe, unstable without the math; one human carries partial equations in his head (implanted by ancient researchers), and every power in the Territories would kill or clone him for them.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 The Peacekeepers

**In brief:** Mercenary armada turned de facto government: Sebaceans raised from birth in fleet service, forbidden permanent attachment, enforcing "order" as both ideology and income line.

- **Discipline culture:** failure is punished by death or "reversal"; loyalty runs to unit and code, never to species idealism.
- **Breeding program:** controlled reproduction; emotional attachment is contraband.
- **Threat profile:** command carriers erase non-compliant planets. Their weakness: rigidity, creativity is insubordination, which makes them predictable to anyone creative enough to be a fugitive.

### 6.1a Peacekeeper Rank & Command Structure

| Rank/Role | Function |
|---|---|
| Commandant | Fleet-level command; answers to High Command |
| Captain (Command Carrier) | Carrier authority — absolute aboard ship |
| Officer cadre | Pilots, techs, commandos — all Sebacean, all bred or conscripted |
| Peacekeeper Marines | Ground enforcement; boarding actions |

**Discipline doctrine:** failure is punished by death, neural-block "reversal," or reassignment to suicide duty. Attachment (love, family) is contraband by regulation because it competes with the chain of command **[W]**.

### 6.1b The Aurora Chair

**What:** Peacekeeper interrogation device: drains and projects a victim's memories as replayed imagery while the subject experiences every recollection again, including the ones the mind buried.
**Where:** Command carriers, interrogation facilities.
**Cost of contact:** Memory theft with courtroom applications, the chair's output is admissible truth extracted at the cost of the self. Victims leave with holes where certainty used to be.

### 6.2 The Scarran Imperium

Heat-absorbing empire with personal firepower: Scarrans project searing thermal energy, shrug off pulse fire, and classify all other species as subjects or meat. Sebacean heat delirium (incapacitation at high temperature) makes humans specifically prey. Their politics are slow, patient, and genocidal on schedule.

### 6.3 The Nebari Establishment

The quiet conquest: mind-cleansing "re-education" spreads conformity person by person; established agents don't know they're agents until activated. No open war, just appointments.

### 6.4 Bounty Economy

Every fugitive has a price and every port a broker. Bounty hunters (Zenetan pirates, Vorcarian blood trackers) are weather, not war, but weather kills too.

## PART SEVEN: COMMAND AND ORGANIZATION (ABOARD A FUGITIVE SHIP)

```
MOYA'S CREW — no chain of command, only survival consensus
   Pilot (ship's voice)  ·  D'Argo (de facto military lead)
   Aeryn (PK tactics)  ·  Rygel (sovereign pretensions + contacts)
   Chiana (streets)  ·  Jool/Zhaan/other cycles' specialists
   Crichton (wormhole knowledge — the reason everyone hunts them)
        |
   TALYN & CRAIS — allied gunship, independent command
```

**In brief:** The crew is a committee of enemies-turned-family; leadership shifts by crisis type. Peacekeeper discipline would have killed them months ago; the committee keeps them alive and at each other's throats.

- **Pilot/Moya veto:** the ship and her navigator can refuse courses that harm the leviathan, the only hard authority aboard.
- **Crichton clause:** wormhole knowledge makes him simultaneously the most valuable and most endangered person aboard; protection duty rotates.

## PART EIGHT: TERRITORIES BESTIARY

### 8.0 The Ancients (Eidolons)

**What:** Illusion-casting elder race that tests travelers with simulated realities before granting real help.
**Cost of contact:** You may still be in the test.

### 8.2 The Builders

**What:** The species who created leviathans as living ships; they consider their creations children, not property.
**Where:** Beyond contact.
**Cost of contact:** Freeing a slave species has consequences for whoever bred it.

### 8.3 Neural Clones ("Harvey" class implants)

**What:** Personality-copy chips implanted in hostages' brains, a Peacekeeper interrogation officer living in your head, complete with his opinions.
**Where:** One known host.
**Cost of contact:** Sanity tax. The clone argues well; he's had your whole personality to study.

### 8.4 Scorpius's Cooling Suit

**What:** Life-support armor for a Scarran-Sebacean hybrid whose own body heat would cook him, worn by the Peacekeeper science directorate's most brilliant, most vengeful officer.
**Cost of contact:** He cannot be intimidated by anything less than the rod (his own torture device), which is why he uses it on everyone else.

### 8.5 The Diagnosan Standard

**What:** Medicine perfected: any injury survivable if you reach one, payment negotiable, bedside manner absent.
**Cost of contact:** They find other species biologically repugnant. Gratitude confuses them.

### 8.6 Wormholes

**What:** Natural space-time tunnels; stable transit requires mathematics one human carries involuntarily.
**Where:** Everywhere and nowhere.
**Cost of contact:** Whoever masters wormhole weapons ends all wars forever, one way or the other **[W]**.
