# GENE RODDENBERRY'S ANDROMEDA: TECHNICAL MANUAL

**HIGH GUARD / SYSTEMS COMMONWEALTH REFERENCE, FIRST EDITION**

*Thirteenth entry in the collected set. Prepared in the tradition of the Star Trek Writers'/DS9 Technical Manuals: an in-universe reference consolidating established canon, with clearly marked engineering extrapolation.*

---

## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This manual provides the detailed technical background that stories aboard the *Andromeda Ascendant* and across the Three Galaxies sometimes need. It supplements character work, this setting's drama is restoring a fallen civilization one system at a time, and every technology here is either a relic of that civilization or a weapon built to prevent its return.

**Edition pin.** This First Edition reflects status quo as of **CY 10087, Captain Dylan Hunt's third year commanding the recovered *Andromeda Ascendant*, rebuilding the Systems Commonwealth one signature at a time** (see §1.5). The Seefra cycles are beyond this edition's scope.

**Conventions used throughout:**

|---|---|
| *(no marker)* | Established by broadcast canon |
| **[E]** | Extrapolation where canon was silent |
| **[W]** | Writer's latitude note |

Canon wins; log conflicts in Appendix C.

## PART ONE: THE THREE GALAXIES

### 1.0 Introduction

For over ten thousand years the Systems Commonwealth, founded by the Vedrans of Tarn-Vedra, governed three galaxies as the largest civilization in history. Then its own elite soldiers betrayed it in a single coordinated night. Three hundred years of dark age followed: worlds falling to Nietzschean prides, Magog swarms devouring the Rim, and the High Guard reduced to legend.

Then a salvage ship found a captain frozen in time at the event horizon of a black hole, still commanding a warship from before the Fall. Every system in this manual exists because of that discovery.

**In brief:** The setting is post-apocalyptic restoration: everything worth having is ancient, broken, and waiting for someone worthy of it.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Three Galaxies (Milky Way, Andromeda, Triangulum) — Commonwealth former territory |
| Flagship | *Andromeda Ascendant* (Glorious Heritage-class heavy cruiser) — last fully operational High Guard capital ship |
| Powers | Nietzschean prides, Magog swarms, Restorian raiders, Perseid remnant administration, Than-Thre-Kull holdings, scattered Commonwealth successor states |
| Posture | Commonwealth restoration project: signatures gathered, systems rejoined, enemies inherited |

Three facts define strategy:

1. **The Fall was an inside job.** The Nietzscheans were the Commonwealth's own elite soldiers, any restoration must answer what changed.
2. **Slipstream is the only road.** All FTL runs through the slipstream, navigable only by organic instinct, whoever controls pilots controls movement.
3. **Legitimacy is the scarcest resource.** A thousand factions claim the Commonwealth's mantle; Dylan Hunt carries the original's actual credentials, and that paper trail outranks armadas in negotiation.

**[W]** Andromeda stories are about rebuilding while under fire: each restored world strengthens the whole but paints a target on it. Keep restoration expensive.

### 1.2 Consolidated Timeline

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| ~CY 7000s | Vedran era: slipstream discovered via Tarn-Vedra; Commonwealth grows from matriarchal founding species to tri-galactic empire, then democracy |
| CY 9000s+ | Golden age: High Guard patrols nine hundred thousand worlds; Nietzscheans engineered as soldier caste |
| CY 9781 | **The Fall:** Nietzschean prides betray the Commonwealth simultaneously; Tarn-Vedra severed from slipstream; central authority collapses overnight |
| CY 9781–9783 | High Guard stand: *Andromeda Ascendant* holds the Hephaistos system against pride fleets until Magog swarm attack; Dylan Hunt lost at black hole event horizon, ship frozen in time |
| 300 years of chaos | Prides war among themselves; Magog worldship devours colony worlds; Restorians hunt refugees |
| CY 10087 | Eureka Maru salvage crew recovers Hunt and ship; Commonwealth restoration declared. **Edition pin: third year of restoration** |

### 1.3 Assessment of Major Powers

*(Full dossiers in Part Six.)*

**The Nietzschean Prides.** Genetically engineered super-soldiers who outbred their makers and inherited the ruins. Organized into prides (Drago-Kazov paramount) bound by clan loyalty and breeding contracts rather than governments. Individually superior, collectively fractious, their great strength and fatal flaw are the same trait.

**The Magog.** Swarm-species horror of the Rim: world-devouring raids, eggs laid in living hosts, and a god-thing called the Spirit of the Abyss behind their migrations. Their worldship is a moving apocalypse.

**Restorians.** Pirate confederation hunting refugees and salvagers, born of the Fall's refugee crisis, now a permanent institution of prey-taking.

**Perseids.** Former Commonwealth administrators maintaining bureaucratic islands of order; they governed well once and remember how.

**Than-Thre-Kull.** Crystalline collective minds of the inner territories; stable, alien, and quietly powerful.

**The Systems Commonwealth (restored).** Dylan Hunt's signature-gathering project: member worlds rejoining a government that currently consists of one starship, her crew, and increasingly credible paperwork.
### 1.4 Founding Technologies

**In brief:** Three systems define the Commonwealth era and its fall:

1. **Slipstream**, FTL through a tunnelled dimension navigated by *organic instinct*, not computation. Ships without pilots are stranded; AIs fly poorly; Nietzscheans engineered for spatial intuition fly brilliantly.
2. **Artificial gravity & inertial damping**, mature Commonwealth engineering (no rotating sections anywhere).
3. **Nova bombs**, slippstream-delivered devices that collapse a star into a black hole. Nine aboard a Glorious Heritage-class cruiser. The Commonwealth's final argument, used twice in recorded history **[W]**.

| Restoration | ~dozens of worlds signed **[E]**; Commonwealth charter re-ratified in progress |

### 2.01 The Ship's AI & Avatar

**In brief:** Commonwealth heavy cruisers carry full sentient intelligences. *Andromeda*'s AI manifests three ways simultaneously: holographic projections anywhere aboard, a manufactured humanoid avatar walking the corridors, and the ship herself. She fought her last battle alone for three hundred subjective seconds frozen at the event horizon; she does not handle abandonment well **[W]**.

- **AI authority:** the captain commands the crew; the AI commands the ship. Mutual override exists at the highest levels, a deliberate Commonwealth check against either going rogue.
- **Avatar combat capability:** superhuman strength/durability by design; the avatar can operate independently away from the hull.
- **Ramming doctrine note:** an AI-crewed ship can execute maneuvers no organic crew would survive, used exactly once per crisis, never casually **[W]**.

### 2.1 Defensive & Offensive Systems

- **AP cannons:** four antiproton turret banks, capital-scale energy fire.
- **Kinetic missiles:** massed launch cells; salvo doctrine against pride fleets.

- **High-tension armor:** reactive plating; the ship tanked a pride fleet for hours at Hephaistos.
- **Nova bombs:** star-collapsing strategic devices, see §8.3.

## PART THREE: SLIPSTREAM OPERATIONS

### 3.0 The Slipstream

**In brief:** FTL travel tunnels through slipstream, a dimension of probability where the route is *chosen by instinct*, not computed. The pilot "feels" the correct path among infinite possibilities; wrong choices tear ships apart or strand them.

**Operational rules:**

- **Organic pilots only.** AIs and computers navigate slipstream badly, Commonwealth AI-assisted jumps were possible but risky; pure machine navigation is effectively impossible **[W]**.
- **Nietzschean advantage:** engineered spatial intuition makes prides natural pilots, one more reason the Fall succeeded and restoration struggles.
- **No communication through slipstream:** messages ride ships. Command authority across three galaxies died with real-time comms; every restored world governs itself between visits.
- **Slipstream cores are irreplaceable post-Fall:** new cores require Commonwealth-era industry nobody still has. Every working core belongs to someone's fleet, museum, or grave.

**[W]** Slipstream keeps this setting honest: no reinforcements arrive on schedule, no empire can react quickly, and every rescue is flown by someone who chose to be there.

## PART FOUR: VESSELS

**Recognition reference:** see plate `Andromeda - Ship Recognition Plate.png` (companion file).

### 4.1 High Guard / Commonwealth Vessels

#### Glorious Heritage-Class Heavy Cruiser

See §2.0, the class flagship defines the type: sentient AI, heavy antiproton armament, Nova capability, thousands-strong wartime complement. Eight sisters of the class survived the Fall scattered **[E]**; recovering them is ongoing plot currency.

#### Wrath of Achilles-Class Battleship

Pre-Fall battleship line **[canon name, specs E]**: heavier than Glorious Heritage, slower, built for fleet-line combat in the Nietzschean War's opening years. At least one hull (*Wrath of Achilles*) operated post-Fall under remnant High Guard command.

#### Siege Perilous-Class **[E]**

Superbattleship lineage referenced in High Guard records; flagship-scale. Treat as historical legend until encountered, which would be an event.

### 4.2 Independent Vessels

#### Eureka Maru

§2.2, the crew's second home.

#### Nietzschean Pride Fleets

Prides fly converted High Guard remnants plus their own builds **[E]**: fast attack cruisers optimized for wolfpack ambush, fighter screens, and troop transport for planet-seizing. Drago-Kazov fields the largest concentration.

### 4.3 Threat Vessels

#### Magog Swarm Ships

Small organic-alloy craft operating in swarms of hundreds from the worldship: boarding-first doctrine, Magog don't conquer worlds, they *infest* them, laying eggs in survivors.

#### Restorian Warships **[E]**

Refitted freighters and cruisers flying Restorian colors: interdiction and execution raids against refugee convoys. Individually unremarkable; institutionally permanent.

### 5.2 Personal Weapons

- **Force lance:** the Commonwealth's signature sidearm, a smart staff that retracts to baton size, fires plasma darts and kinetic bolts, and delivers non-lethal stun settings. It is also a melee weapon; High Guard training treats it as one.
- **Faster-than-light projectile pistols **[E]**: Nietzschean/mercenary sidearms of post-Fall manufacture.
- **Grenade launchers & plasma rifles:** heavy personal fire; scarce, salvaged.

### 5.3 Strategic Materials & Systems

- **Slipstream cores** — irreplaceable FTL organs (§3.0); the galaxy's most stolen objects.
- **Nova bombs** — nine exist on this hull; each is a policy decision with a trigger.
- **Commonwealth-era AI cores** — sentient intelligences survive as museum relics or active legends; recovering one is recruiting a person.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 The Nietzschean Prides

**In brief:** Genetically engineered super-soldiers bred by the Commonwealth as its warrior caste, who calculated their masters' decline to the day and betrayed them simultaneously across three galaxies. Post-Fall they inherited everything and have spent three centuries proving inheritance without institutions fails.

**More:**

- **Culture:** Drago Museveni's genome is their scripture; prides (Drago-Kazov paramount) are family-clan-states bound by breeding contracts. Personal honor is real and weaponized.
- **Military profile:** superior strength/speed/endurance; engineered tactical instinct; pride fleets favor ambush and overwhelming first strikes.
- **Weakness:** endless internal war. A pride's word binds it absolutely, which makes treaties possible, and betrayal profitable enough that treaties stay rare.

**Engagement rules:** Never fight a pride fleet at its chosen time. Always know which pride an individual forswore before trusting him. Remember: a Nietzschean's loyalty is to his genes' future, not your flag.

### 6.1 The Magog & The Abyss

**In brief:** Rim-born swarm species: raiders who eat populations alive as incubators. Behind them stands the Spirit of the Abyss, a dark intelligence the Commonwealth never catalogued, directing the swarm toward extinguishing order itself.

- **Worldship:** a hollowed planetoid moving under its own power; millions of Magog; the invasion's capital.
- **Doctrine:** swarm ships board and seed eggs; no negotiation has ever changed this.
- **The Abyss:** manifests through possessed hosts **[W]**; treat every unexplained coordinated evil in the Territories as potentially signed work.

### 6.2 Restorians

Refugee-turned-raider confederation hunting the lanes the Fall created. Their grievance is legitimate; their method is murder. Restoration-era policy: amnesty talks and convoy defense simultaneously.

## PART EIGHT: A THREE-GALAXY BESTIARY

### 8.0 The Spirit of the Abyss

**What:** An ancient dark intelligence directing the Magog and, increasingly, everything that hates the new Commonwealth.
**Where:** Wherever hope organizes successfully.
**Cost of contact:** It doesn't negotiate; it recruits, consumes, or erases.

### 8.2 Nova Bombs

**What:** Nine devices that turn stars into black holes via slipstream insertion into stellar mass.
**Where:** *Andromeda*'s shielded magazine.
**Cost of contact:** Each use deletes a solar system from every future history. Both historical uses ended wars. Neither use slept well afterward.

### 8.3 The Route of Ages **[W]**

**What:** A mythic slipstream passage said to lead anywhere, including places that answer prayers.
**Where:** Unmapped; finds those it chooses.
**Cost of contact:** Passage is one-way and the fare is what you were.

### 8.4 Tarn-Vedra

**What:** The lost capital, severed from slipstream at the Fall, home of the Vedrans, location unknown for three centuries.
**Where:** Off the map by definition.
**Cost of contact:** Finding it ends the setting's central mystery. Guard accordingly **[W]**.
