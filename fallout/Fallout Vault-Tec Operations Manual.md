## ABOUT THIS MANUAL

Congratulations, valued applicant! You have been selected for residency in a Vault-Tec underground shelter: the last word in post-nuclear living. This manual covers facility operations, occupant responsibilities, and the American can-do spirit that will see our great nation through any unpleasantness.

**Document status.** Printed 2077 CE, weeks before the Great War (October 23, 2077; duration: approximately two hours). Distribution: selected vaults. This copy carries post-War annotation markers (**[W]**) added by surviving archivists.

## PART ONE: THE AMERICAN DREAM, UNDERGROUND

### 1.0 Introduction

In the event of total atomic annihilation, Vault-Tec shelters preserve America's greatest asset: its people. Each vault is a self-contained civilization rated for indefinite underground habitation. Your Overseer is your mayor, your employer, and your guardian. Trust the Overseer. Trust Vault-Tec.

**[W]** The Vaults were never shelters first. Vault-Tec contracted with shadow clients to run *social experiments* on sealed populations: 122 public vaults, each with a variable, most with a twist. The door was real. The purpose on the brochure was not.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation (Post-War)

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Post-nuclear North American continent ("the Wasteland") |
| Population centers | Vaults (sealed or opened), settlements, ruins |
| Powers | New California Republic (expansionist democracy), Brotherhood of Steel (tech-hoarding knightly order), Caesar's Legion (slaver empire, East), raider bands, independent towns |
| Hazards | Radiation, mutated fauna, Super Mutants, Feral Ghouls, raiders, and every Vault that opened wrong |

Three facts define the Wasteland:

1. **The bombs were the beginning, not the end.** Two hours of war created two centuries of consequences.
2. **Pre-War technology is the treasure map.** Power armor, energy weapons, and working Vaults decide who rules.
3. **Every Vault opened tells you what Vault-Tec thought people were for.**
### 1.2 Consolidated Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2050s | Resource Wars begin; Europe/Middle East nuclear exchange; United States annexes Canada |
| 2066–2077 | Sino-American War; Anchorage Reclamation (2077, T-51b power armor decisive) |
| 2077, Oct 23 | **The Great War.** Two hours of exchange. Vault doors close |
| 2090s–2160s | First vaults open (Vault 13's water crisis forces the surface); Shady Sands founded; Master's Super Mutant army rises and falls |

| 2200s–2287 | Brotherhood of Steel expansion; Enclave remnant wars; East Coast developments; the Commonwealth's Institute revealed; **Edition pin: 2287, the Sole Survivor emerges from Vault 111** |

### 1.3 Assessment of Powers

**New California Republic (NCR).** The Wasteland's largest democracy: taxes, laws, an army, and all the growing pains of old America replayed at survival scale.

**Brotherhood of Steel.** Technology-preservation knightly order: power armor, vertibirds, and a catechism about who deserves advanced tech (answer: them).

**The Institute (Commonwealth).** Post-War boogeyman made real: synths (biosynthetic replicants), teleportation, and a scientific council deciding humanity's replacement schedule **[W]**.

**Raider bands & Super Mutants.** The Wasteland's weather: seasonal, violent, survivable with walls.

### 1.5 Status Quo Snapshot (2287 CE)

| Domain | Status |

| NCR | Continental republic stretching from the Boneyard to the Mojave |
| Vault legacy | Most vaults opened, failed, or became towns; sealed ones remain unopened stories |
| Pre-War tech | Power armor and energy cells still decide battles |

## PART TWO: VAULT OPERATIONS

### 2.0 Facility Overview

**In brief:** A standard Vault is a self-contained underground township: 50–1,000 occupant capacity **[E]**, gear-door blast seal rated for direct nuclear strike, closed-cycle air/water/power, hydroponics farms, and a social order run by an Overseer with absolute authority and a sealed envelope of instructions from Vault-Tec.

### 2.01 Occupant Systems

- **Pip-Boy 3000 (Personal Information Processor):** wrist-mounted computer issued to every resident: stats, inventory, radio (Vault-Tec broadcasts only, pre-War stations persist as static and miracles), map display.
- **G.O.A.T. (Generalized Occupational Aptitude Test):** career assignment exam taken at age 16. Your result decides your job for life. Retesting discouraged **[W]**.
- **Rations & water:** processed from recycled systems; quality is a budget line the Overseer manages.
- **Power armor displays & energy weapons stay in the armory**, which stays Overseer-locked. See §2.02 for why some vaults regretted that.
### 2.02 The Vault Experiment Program **[W: this is the manual's dark heart]**

**Archivist's note:** The brochure said "shelter." The contracts with the Enclave said "laboratory." Most vaults were social experiments with human populations as instruments. Selected entries from the Vault-Tec experiment registry:

| Vault | Official purpose | Actual variable |
|---|---|---|
| Vault 13 | Standard shelter | Water chip lifespan deliberately short: forced surface contact on a schedule |
| Vault 101 | Standard shelter | Door never opens. Ever. Overseer succession by any means |
| Vault 111 | Cryogenic preservation | Cryo pods for select residents; everyone else was furniture |
| Vault 112 | Virtual reality refuge | Occupants locked in a simulation run by a madman |
| Vault 108 | Cloning facility | Population of clones, all named Gary |
| Vault 11 | Civic duty | Annual sacrifice election: refuse and the vault punishes everyone |
| Vault 75 | Education | Children processed for "genetic improvement"; adults did not survive intake |
| Vault 95 | Rehabilitation | Chem-addicts cured, then offered the drugs again at peak misery |
| Vault 106 | Standard shelter | Hallucinogenic gas in the air supply |
| Vault 118 | Luxury suites | Ghoulish residents; power ran to the wrong floors |

The control vaults (e.g., Vault 76) existed to be comparisons: opened on schedule, no variable. Their populations became the Wasteland's founding stock.

**[W]** Every vault is a short story premise: a sealed society, one twisted variable, and a door that opens when the data is complete. The horror is bureaucratic.

## PART THREE: WASTELAND SURVIVAL OPERATIONS

### 3.1 Radiation Discipline

- Rad-Away (chelating IV) and Rad-X (pre-exposure chem) are survival currency.
- Irradiated water heals nothing and feeds everything: drink filtered or suffer mutation risk (ghouls are the endpoint of chronic exposure, not a guarantee).
- Geiger counters are standard Pip-Boy attachments. Trust the clicking.

## PART FIVE: FACTIONS AND DIPLOMACY

### 5.1 Brotherhood of Steel

Tech-preservation military order descended from pre-War Army elements. Doctrine: advanced technology in untrained hands caused the Great War; the Brotherhood collects it like fire crews collect matches. Ranks mirror knightly orders (Initiate → Elder). Their air fleet and power armor make them a superpower with a monastery's population.

### 5.2 The Institute (Commonwealth dossier)

Post-War scientific underground beneath Boston: teleportation, synths (Generation 3 biosynthetic humans indistinguishable from people), and a governing council that replaced surface humanity's future with its own design program. Synth liberation politics are the Commonwealth's civil rights war **[W]**.

### 5.3 New California Republic

Democratic federation: elected president, two-house legislature, taxation, and an army fighting old America's problems at new-America scale. The NCR proves democracy survives the bomb, and that it inherits every flaw democracy ever had.

## PART SIX: HAZARD BESTIARY

### 6.0 The Great War Legacy

**What:** Two hours of exchange left 77 years of winter-less radiation seasons, dead oceans, and continents of ruins holding live warheads.
**Cost of contact:** Chronic everywhere. Rad-X is a meal planning decision.

### 6.2 FEV and Super Mutants

**What:** Forced Evolutionary Virus converting humans into sterile giants of varying intelligence; the Master's army (West) and the Institute's experiments (East) both manufacture them.
**Cost of contact:** Conversion is irreversible. The mutants remember being human, sometimes.

### 6.3 Ghoul Continuum

**What:** Radiation-scarred humans: feral ghouls (mindless aggression) retain memory fragments; normal ghouls (centenarian survivors) are fully sapient citizens facing two centuries of prejudice.
**Cost of contact:** Prejudice, mostly. Ferals bite.

### 6.4 The Enclave Question **[W]**

**What:** The pre-War government's continuity-of-command remnant: pure-blood doctrine, vertibird fleets, and genocidal filtration plans for "mutie" populations.
**Status:** Defeated twice, never quite gone.

## PART SEVEN: VAULT CULTURE AND GOVERNANCE

- **The Overseer:** executive authority per charter; experiment vaults gave the role sealed directives from Corporate. The tension between Overseer instructions and occupant survival is every vault's founding drama.
- **Succession:** appointment, election, assassination, or cloning, depending on vault. Document your succession before someone documents it for you.
- **Opening day:** the moment a vault recontacts the Wasteland is its graduation and its exposure. Some thrived (76, 8), some collapsed on the threshold (13 nearly), some should never have opened at all (112 was already too late).
