# THE SPACING GUILD
## NAVIGATOR'S OPERATING HANDBOOK

*Eighteenth entry in the collected set; house-style variant: authored as Guild internal doctrine. The Guild sells certainty; this manual explains what it actually buys.*

---

## ABOUT THIS HANDBOOK

This handbook instructs Guild Navigators and steersmen in the conduct of foldspace travel, spice discipline, and the political neutrality that keeps the Imperium moving. It supplements judgment. The Spacing Guild holds a monopoly on interstellar transport because its Navigators are the only beings who can fold space safely, and this monopoly is the single most important fact of Imperial politics.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **the reign of Emperor Shaddam IV, prior to the Arrakis upheaval (Atreides accession)**.

## PART ONE: THE IMPERIAL THEATER

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Power | Domain | Relationship to the Guild |
|---|---|---|
| Padishah Emperor | Imperial throne, Sardaukar terror troops | Customer and hostage-holder |
| Landsraad Great Houses | Fiefdoms and private armies | Customers bound by Kanly (vendetta law) |
| CHOAM | Economic combine | Profit partner |
| Spacing Guild | All interstellar transport | Monopoly; politically neutral by charter |
| Bene Gesserit Sisterhood | Breeding program, Truthsayers | Uncomfortable equals |

Three facts define the Imperium:

1. **Who controls the spice controls the universe.** Melange extends life, unlocks prescience, and is the only substance that creates Navigators. It exists on one planet.
2. **Travel is monopoly, communication is not.** Guild ships move everything; messages ride Guild couriers too.
3. **Neutrality is enforced by dependence.** Every Great House needs the Guild more than the Guild needs any one house.

### 2.0 The Heighliner

**In brief:** A Guild heighliner is a city-sized starship carrying hundreds of smaller vessels between stars by folding space. Canon does not fix its length precisely; descriptions place them at many kilometers, holding fleets internally like a harbor.

| Complement | Thousands of crew, passenger berths for houses' retinues |

### 2.01 The Foldspace Jump

1. Navigator ascends the tank chamber and immerses in concentrated melange gas.
2. Prescience awakens: the Navigator *sees* the safe path through folded space.
3. Space folds; the heighliner and everything docked within arrives at destination.
4. Misfold without a Navigator: historically catastrophic. No exceptions to Navigator requirement.

**[W]** Foldspace travel has no transit time worth narrating. The drama is in the Navigator's vision: they see futures, including their own death paths, and steer around them.

## PART FIVE: THE SPICE ECONOMY

**Melange:** geriatric spice, cinnamon-scented, blue-within-blue eye stain with chronic use. Effects:

1. Extends human lifespan by decades.
2. Unlocks prescient awareness in trained minds (Navigators, and some Sisters).
3. Is fatally addictive: withdrawal is death.

**Supply:** melange exists only on Arrakis, produced by sandworm lifecycle events nobody has replicated off-world.

**Guild doctrine:** the spice must flow. Guild prescience monitors all futures for threats to supply; the Guild's political interventions across ten millennia are all, ultimately, logistics.

**[W]** The Guild's neutrality ends exactly where spice supply is threatened. Everything else is negotiable, including emperors.

## PART SIX: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.1 Sandworms of Arrakis

**In brief:** Shai-Hulud: kilometer-scale sandworms guarding melange deposits. Vibrations attract them; shields drive them to frenzy (shield/lasgun interaction detonates like atomics).

**Engagement rules:** Do not walk with rhythm. Carry thumper decoys. Ride them the Fremen way only after you understand what that costs.

### 6.2 Bene Gesserit Sisterhood

**In brief:** The breeding-program Sisterhood: Truthsayers who read lies, prana-bindu body control, the Voice that commands obedience. They planted religions for their own contingencies (the Missionaria Protectiva) and are two generations from their own prescient goal (the Kwisatz Haderach).
**Relations:** Equal rivals. The Guild respects nothing else in the Imperium more.

### 6.3 The Harkonnen Question

House Harkonnen holds Arrakis at pin date under fief: brutality as extraction policy, gladiatorial cruelty as management style, and a feud with Atreides sanctioned as Kanly under the Great Convention. Their fall and return bracket the pin era **[W]**.

### 6.4 The Fremen

**In brief:** Deep-desert people adapted beyond Outsider comprehension: stillsuits recycling body water, worm-riding as transport, and a messianic expectation (Lisan al-Gaib) the Missionaria Protectiva accidentally seeded centuries ago.
**Assessment:** underestimated by everyone except the desert. That will change.

## PART EIGHT: BESTIARY OF THE IMPERIUM

### 8.0 Holtzman Effect

**What:** The physics substrate of the age: suspensors (gravity negation), shields (slow-penetrating defense), foldspace fields.
**Cost of contact:** Shields plus lasguns detonate like atomics. Every soldier knows; every battlefield fears the madman who tests it.

### 8.2 The Voice

**What:** Bene Gesserit direct-command modulation compelling obedience in nervous systems.
**Cost of contact:** Your own body obeys someone else. Training resists; innocence does not.

### 8.3 Kanly

**What:** Formal vendetta law governing Great House feuds: declared, bounded, ritualized war.
**Cost of contact:** Kanly makes feuds survivable. Breaking Kanly makes you the next common enemy.

### 8.4 Prescience Blindness

**What:** Certain bloodlines and substances hide their futures from Navigators and Seers alike.
**Where:** On file since Muad'Dib's line emerged **[post-pin W]**.
**Cost of contact:** For an institution built on seeing, blindness is both terror and opportunity.
