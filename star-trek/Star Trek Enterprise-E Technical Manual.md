# STAR TREK: U.S.S. ENTERPRISE NCC-1701-E TECHNICAL MANUAL

**SOVEREIGN-CLASS STARSHIP, FEDERATION STARFLEET**

*Twentieth entry in the collected set; house-style variant: written as a Starfleet Corps of Engineers orientation supplement. Dedicated respectfully alongside the Okuda/Sternbach originals on the same shelf.*

---

## ABOUT THIS MANUAL

This supplement documents the Sovereign-class starship *Enterprise* NCC-1701-E as configured following the Dominion War and the Shinzon incident (2379). It assumes familiarity with Federation starship fundamentals covered in the owner's existing technical literature, and extends them to this class.

**Edition pin.** Current as of **2379 CE**, following the *Enterprise*-E's return from Romulan space after the Reman coup attempt.

---

### 1.0 Introduction

The Dominion War ended in 2375 after two years of carnage that reshaped every fleet in the Alpha Quadrant. Starfleet spent 2375–2379 rebuilding, and the Sovereign class became the template: war-tested systems wrapped in an exploration hull, because Starfleet never stops being both.

## PART FOUR: THREAT DOSSIERS

### 4.1 The Borg Collective

Post-2373 assessment: the collective remains the Federation's existential threat. Enterprise-E engagement history includes temporal intervention at First Contact. Current Borg strategic posture fragmented post-Unimatrix events **[W beyond scope]**.

### 4.2 Romulan Star Empire

Reman coup under Shinzon exposed Romulan political fragility; thalaron radiation weapons demonstrated genocide-scale capability aboard a single ship. The Bassen Rift battle validated cloak-defeating rift tactics. Post-2379 Romulan succession is unresolved **[E]**.

### 4.3 The Son'a / Ba'ku Affair

Insurrection-era operation: Federation-adjacent misconduct exposed and refused by this crew. Logged as precedent for orders-refusal ethics cases at the Academy.

## PART SIX: BESTIARY

### 6.0 Temporal Incursion Doctrine

**What:** Borg temporal vortex technology demonstrated at Earth 2373.
**Doctrine:** pursue through vortices only with full crew consent; changing one's own past is the heaviest bill in this set.

### 6.1 Thalaron Radiation

**What:** Romulan-developed biogenic radiation: instant organic tissue destruction at planetary scale from a single device.
**Where:** Scimitar wreckage; proliferation watch active.
**Cost of contact:** A single emitter is a species-level threat.

### 6.2 The Briar Patch

**What:** Metreon-cloud region: sensor-hostile, metaphasic-radiation pockets, Son'a industrial presence.
**Cost of contact:** Ships that hide there find things older than the Federation.
