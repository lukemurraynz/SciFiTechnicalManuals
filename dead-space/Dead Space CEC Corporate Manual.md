## ABOUT THIS DIRECTIVE

This document consolidates fleet specifications, planetary extraction procedures, and personnel policy for Concordance Extraction Corporation assets operating under EarthGov charter. Crew members are reminded that CEC property is the backbone of human civilization, and that resource recovery is not merely an industry but a duty owed to the species.

**Edition pin.** Current as of the **Aegis VII incident review cycle (2508 CE)**. Prior directives superseded. The Corporation expresses condolences to the families involved and reminds all personnel that speculation about causes is both premature and actionable.

## PART ONE: THE COMMERCIAL THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

Humanity outgrew its cradle and then its solar system. The Resource Wars ended because planet cracking ended scarcity: Concordance Extraction Corporation vessels crack barren planets open and strip them to the core. Every colony breathing easy owes its air to a CEC hull number.

**In brief:** We do not mine planets. We harvest them.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Sol System, EarthGov colonial space, deep-field claims |
| Fleet | Planet Cracker-class vessels (5+ active **[E]**), refinery barges, support craft |
| Regulator | EarthGov: charter oversight, quarantine authority, military arm (USM forces) |
| Market position | CEC is the extraction industry |

Defining facts:

1. **Planet cracking works.** Thirty-five successful cracks aboard the flagship alone. The alternative is the Resource Wars everyone agreed to forget.
2. **EarthGov needs us more than we need EarthGov.** Charter renewals are formalities. Quarantine authority is where they push back.
3. **The Church of Unitology rides our ships.** Their membership includes our crews, our officers, and people above Corporate pay grade. Personnel policy treats faith as private; manifests treat it as data.

### 1.3 Assessment of Powers

**Concordance Extraction Corporation.** The extraction monopoly. Fleet, foundries, colonial infrastructure contracts, and the political weight of feeding humanity.

**EarthGov.** Colonial federal authority: military (USM forces), quarantine power, and a standing interest in anything that threatens the species. EarthGov warships outrank Corporate security by design.

**Church of Unitology.** Fastest-growing faith in human space. Doctrine centers on the Markers as divine instruments and "Convergence" as salvation. The Church's reach inside CEC crews is extensive, well-funded, and documented in manifests the Corporation does not audit **[W]**.

**The Marker situation.** See Part Six §6.1. Personnel are reminded that unauthorized discussion of Marker-related phenomena violates standing quarantine directives.

### 2.02 Planet Crack Procedure

1. Survey team confirms target viability (barren rock preferred; colony clearance required for inhabited claims).
2. Colony installs gravity tethers at the harvest seam.
3. Fracture array fires: a planet-sized chunk is broken from the body.
4. Tethers haul the chunk into orbit and into the Mining Deck's maw.
5. Onboard processing strips ore; refined stock loads to cargo.

**Failure modes:** tether cascade failure (Wanat Disaster, 2497: total asset loss), orbital decay under load, and fracture-seam misreads. Corporate review notes that prior to Aegis VII only one operation in three dozen met with less-than-optimal results. The review does not count Aegis VII as an extraction failure, because the rock came up fine **[W]**.

## PART THREE: SHOCKPOINT OPERATIONS AND COMMUNICATIONS

**ShockPoint Drive:** FTL transit via shockpoint jumps. Range and precision are proven technology; jump calculations are computer-assisted. First capital-scale fit aboard Ishimura (2446).

**Communications:** EarthGov Relay network handles interstellar traffic. Ships may declare radio silence; CEC policy permits it for operational security, and Aegis VII demonstrated how a silent flagship becomes a legal fiction until someone investigates **[W]**.

## PART FIVE: EQUIPMENT AND PERSONNEL SYSTEMS

### 5.1 The RIG (Resource Integration Gear)

Every crew member wears a RIG: a full-body suit with holographic spine display showing health status, stasis energy, and air reserve. Suit integrity, weapon ammo counts, and credits project into the wearer's vision. The RIG is why headcount after incidents is precise even when survivors are not.

### 5.2 Tools as Weapons (Corporate Policy Notice)

CEC vessels do not carry military weapons. Personnel defend themselves with mining equipment:

| Tool | Original function | Field use |
|---|---|---|
| Plasma Cutter | Precision ore slicing | The standard; vertical/horizontal fire mode |
| Line Gun | Wide-beam cutting | Area denial; mines |
| Pulse Rifle | Security model (P-Sec) | Automatic fire; issued to security only |
| Ripper | Cable-saw blade projection | Devastating at close range |
| Flamethrower | Debris clearing | Swarm suppression |

### 5.3 Stasis and Kinesis Modules

Engineering-grade modules integrated into RIGs:

- **Stasis module:** emits a field slowing objects and machinery for seconds. Rated for industrial accidents. Also rated, as it turns out, against fast-moving biological hazards **[E]**.
- **Kinesis module:** telekinetic grapple for moving heavy salvage. Range: short. Utility: endless.

**[W]** The tools-as-weapons doctrine is the franchise's voice: nothing aboard was designed to fight what the crew faces, and improvisation is the only arsenal that ships on time.

## PART SIX: SECTION 7: BIOHAZARD AND PHENOMENA DOSSIER

*(This section exists because Section 6 was sealed by Corporate review. The numbering is retained so auditors know where to look.)*

### 6.1 The Markers

**In brief:** Alien obelisks emitting a signal field that influences living minds: dementia, hallucination, compulsion, and religious revelation in exposed populations. Black Marker (original, Earth-origin discovery predating the Corporation) and Red Markers (copies) share the signal profile.

**Corporate position:** Markers are mineralogical curiosities of high research value. Recovery operations are authorized when profitable.

**Field reality **[W]**:** exposure cascades through a population as insomnia, violence, mass hysteria, and faith. The Church of Unitology teaches that Markers are divine and their signal is holy. The Church may be right. That possibility is the problem.

### 6.2 Necromorphs

**In brief:** Recombinant dead. The Marker signal restructures necrotic tissue into hostile organisms from whatever corpses are available: crew, patients, family members. Necromorphs are not infected people; they are the corpses of infected people, rebuilt into tools of slaughter.

| Variant | Built from | Threat profile |
|---|---|---|
| Slasher | Single corpse + blade-limb grafts | Standard attacker; fast, flanking |
| Infector | Winged baton-form | Converts fresh corpses mid-battle; priority target |
| Leaper | Limb-optimized recombination | Wall-crawling, lunging |
| Brute | Massed biomass | Charging mini-boss; armor-plated front |
| Pregnant | Swollen carrier | Releases swarms when ruptured **[W]** |
| Guardians / Swarmers | Wall-integrated tissue | Area denial |

**Kill doctrine (mandatory training point):** Necromorphs ignore headshots. Dismemberment stops them: sever the limbs. This single fact separates survivors from statistics, and it is the reason CEC cutting tools outperform military rifles aboard its own ships.

### 6.3 Convergence

**What:** The Marker end-state: mass death followed by mass recombination into something planetary-scale. Aegis VII did not reach Convergence. The Church believes Convergence is salvation.
**Where:** Wherever enough dead meet an active Marker.
**Cost of contact:** Everything the word "everything" covers.

### 6.4 The Church of Unitology (institutional dossier)

A faith founded on a recovered Marker artifact a century before Aegis VII. Structure: centralized church leadership, congregation cells inside every major institution including CEC crews. Doctrine: Markers are the divine mechanism; death in their service is transformation.

The Church's interference in the Aegis VII response is documented in sealed appendices. Personnel policy prohibits discrimination by faith. Manifest policy records it anyway **[W]**.

## PART EIGHT: HAZARD BESTIARY

### 8.0 Marker Dementia

**What:** Progressive psychological deterioration in Marker-exposed populations: insomnia, irritability, hallucinations (dead loved ones are common), homicidal episodes, religious conversion.
**Where:** Anywhere a Marker broadcasts.
**Cost of contact:** The dementia is functional. It moves exposed populations toward killing each other so raw material accumulates near the Marker. The disease is the harvest mechanism.

### 8.2 Gravity Tether Cascade

**What:** Industrial failure mode: tether loss under load drops a planet-cracked fragment back onto the surface below.
**Where:** Extraction sites (Wanat, 2497).
**Cost of contact:** Extinction-of-site scale. Tether inspections are mandatory, logged, and the first thing corner-cutting cuts.

### 8.3 ShockPoint Misjump

**What:** Navigation errors in shockpoint transit strand vessels off-course or damage drive systems irreparably.
**Where:** Deep-space lanes.
**Cost of contact:** Rescue is a business decision. CEC crews update their wills accordingly.

### 8.4 Unitology Aboard Ship

**What:** Not a hazard per Corporate policy. In practice: unregistered Church meetings during quarantines, refusal of medical procedures conflicting with doctrine, and crew loyalty networks that outrank deck chiefs.
**Cost of contact:** Unknown until it is everyone **[W]**.
