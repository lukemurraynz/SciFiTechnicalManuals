## PART ONE — THE KAIJU WAR

### 1.0 Introduction

The first Kaiju came through a portal at the bottom of the Pacific and took six days and three cities' worth of ordnance to kill. It took humanity one look at the corpse's biology to understand two things: these things were engineered, and more were coming. The Jaeger Program is humanity's answer: giant mecha, piloted by two minds fused into one, deployed from coastal fortresses against monsters that scale up every year.

**In brief:** The war is simple. Something on the other side of the ocean floor sends increasingly large creatures through a door. We meet them with robots the size of buildings.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Theater | Pacific Rim coastline; Breach at Challenger Deep |
| Command | Pan Pacific Defense Corps (PPDC) |
| Assets | Active Shatterdomes (Hong Kong primary at pin date); Jaeger fleet reduced to four |
| Enemy | Kaiju: Category I–V bio-weapons; Precursor architects behind the Breach |

Defining facts:

1. **Kaiju scale escalates by category.** Category I tested us. Category IV beats whole fleets. Category V exists.
2. **Jaeger production lost to Wall of Life politics.** The UN defunded the program for coastal walls; walls do not punch back.
3. **The Breach is genetic-keyed.** Only Kaiju DNA passes both ways. Closing it requires taking a nuclear payload *through* it, which requires getting there alive.

### 1.3 Assessment of Forces

**The PPDC.** Multinational military science organization: academies (Kodiak Island), shatterdomes, LOCCENT mission control, K-Science division (kaiju biology), J-Tech (Jaeger engineering).

**The Precursors.** The enemy's engineers: trans-dimensional xenobiologists engineering Kaiju as invasion weapons, category by category, generation by generation. They are patient, they are winning the attrition math, and they are not present on our side of the Breach.

**Kaiju Blue.** Every kill contaminates: Kaiju blood is toxic cyan-blue and vaporizes into an airborne blight when carelessly spilled. A perfect kill poisons a city anyway. Cleanup doctrine exists; "perfect" does not.

## PART TWO — DRIFT PROTOCOL AND SHATTERDOME OPERATIONS

### 2.0 The Drift (Neural Handshake)

**In brief:** Jaegers are too large for one nervous system. Two Rangers pilot as one through the Drift: a neural handshake merging motor control, memory, and instinct. Left hemisphere and right hemisphere of one giant brain.

**Rules of the Drift:**

1. **Do not chase the R.A.B.I.T.** Random Access Brain Impulse Triggers: memories that surface mid-drift. Chasing them seizes the connection. Let them pass.
2. **Familiarity deepens the bond.** Sibling pairs and long partnerships drift deeper. Loss in the pod is loss in the mind.
3. **Solo piloting is possible once.** The neural load nearly kills the pilot. It has been done twice on record. Both pilots earned legends; neither volunteered again lightly.
4. **Kaiju brains can be drifted into.** K-Science uses single-pilot drift with extracted Kaiju tissue to read the Precursor network. This is the bravest and least advisable thing anyone does in this war.

### 2.1 LOCCENT Mission Control

Local command center: tracks Kaiju signatures from the Breach approach, drops Jaegers by helicopter sling, coordinates with civilian authorities, and calls the fight. The Marshal commands from LOCCENT or suits up personally; both have precedent.

## PART THREE — JAEGER ENGAGEMENT DOCTRINE

1. **Fight at the waterline.** Kaiju are amphibious but clumsy ashore; Jaeger armor is thickest for coastal brawl.
2. **Plasmacaster first, blades last.** Kaiju blood is toxic: cutting wounds the city. Plasma burns cauterize.
3. **Dismemberment over headshots.** Kaiju biology distributes function; decapitation is not assumed lethal.
4. **Category discipline:** engagement authorization scales with category. Category IV requires multi-Jaeger deployment. Category V had no doctrine until Slattern.
5. **Nuclear option:** the Breach closes to Kaiju genetics only. A nuclear payload delivered *through* it is the only confirmed closure method.

## PART FOUR — THE JAEGER ROSTER

### 4.1 Gipsy Danger (Mark-3, USA)

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Launched | July 10, 2017 |
| Height / Weight | 79.25 m / 1,980 tons |
| Ratings | Speed 7 · Strength 8 · Armor 6 |
| Power | Nuclear Vortex Turbine (analog Arc-9 reactor): **EMP-immune**, unlike digital Mark-IV/V suites |
| Weapons | I-19 Plasmacaster ×2 · GD6 Chain Sword (retractable) |
| Kills | 9 |

The last analog Jaeger and the war's most famous hull. Rebuilt after Knifehead under the Mark-III Restoration Program (solid iron hull, no alloys). Destroyed at the Breach: self-destruct payload delivered, Slattern dragged down with her **[W]**.

### 4.2 Striker Eureka (Mark-5, Australia)

Fastest Jaeger ever built; eleven kills, the record. Twin chest-launched missiles (K-Stunner), brass-knuckle thrusters. Digital suite: grounded by Leatherback's EMP at Hong Kong. Piloted by the Hansen father-son team.

### 4.3 Cherno Alpha (Mark-1, Russia)

Oldest active Jaeger at pin date: first-generation hull, Tesla fists incinerating at contact, cylinder-head design built like a nuclear plant containment. Crewed by the Kaidanovskys. Lost holding the Hong Kong perimeter against Leatherback and Otachi. Her stand bought the city its evacuation.

### 4.4 Crimson Typhoon (Mark-4, China)

Triple-arm configuration, three pilots (Wei Tang triplets), Thundercloud Formation technique, sawblade limbs. Lost at Hong Kong to Otachi's ambush; plasma chain cannon outranged before close work could matter.

### 4.5 Coyote Tango (Mark-1, Japan)

Cannons-only design for pilots unsuited to melee (radiation history); Stacker Pentecost solo-piloted her against Venom Osaka-scale events. Decommissioned before pin date; his radiation ledger is why.

## PART SIX — THREAT DOSSIERS

### 6.0 Kaiju Classification

| Category | Threat scale | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| I | City nuisance; early war | Trespasser-class |
| II | Serious; requires coordinated response | Yamarashi |
| III | Major engagement; Jaeger-standard opponent | Knifehead |
| IV | Multi-Jaeger threat; EMP/bio special weapons | Leatherback, Otachi, Raiju |
| V | Never seen before Slattern; fleet-scale | Slattern |

Escalation pattern: each category arrives faster than the last. The enemy is iterating.

### 6.1 Kaiju Biology and Behavior

Engineered organisms sharing a genetic signature across categories: grown, not born wild. Behavior shows reconnaissance (early attacks probed defenses), adaptation (counter-weapons appear two attacks later), and coordination (paired assaults). Otachi carried an unborn infant Kaiju; pregnancy was new. Assume the next surprise is already in production.

### 6.2 The Precursors

Trans-dimensional civilization behind the Breach: xenobiologists terraforming Earth by extermination. They see this as scheduled development, not war. Their timetable accelerated when humanity started winning; assume their next iteration is worse than Slattern.

## PART EIGHT — WAR BESTIARY

### 8.0 The Breach

**What:** Trans-dimensional portal at Challenger Deep, genetic-keyed to Kaiju DNA.
**Where:** Pacific floor.
**Cost of contact:** Conventional ordnance bounces off its field. Nuclear delivery through the throat of the thing is the only confirmed closure mechanism, and it requires surviving everything between here and there.

### 8.2 Kaiju Blue Contamination

**What:** Toxic blood event: airborne cyan blight from ruptured kaiju biology.
**Where:** Every kill site.
**Cost of contact:** Cities quarantine districts for months. Blades make it worse; plasma makes it better.

### 8.3 The Drift's Dark Corner

**What:** Memories in the drift can be weapons: a partner who knows your worst day owns your focus during combat. Precursor-influenced minds inside a drift are a standing nightmare scenario.
**Cost of contact:** Trust is equipment. Maintain it like a reactor.

### 8.4 The Wall of Life

**What:** UN-funded coastal barriers replacing Jaeger deployment.
**Cost of contact:** Walls do not punch back. Sydney demonstrated what a Category IV thinks of walls: it climbed over. The program's revival is the pin-date's only good news.
