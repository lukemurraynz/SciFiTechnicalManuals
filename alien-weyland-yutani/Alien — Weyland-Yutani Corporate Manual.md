## PART ONE: THE COMMERCIAL THEATER

### 1.0 Introduction

The Corporation operates mining, towing, terraforming, and colonial development across the Core Systems and the Outer Rim Fringe. Competition is Antares Sequestration Industries, Weyland's rivals in everything, and gravity.

### 1.1 Strategic Situation

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Operating area | Core Systems to Outer Rim Fringe |
| Fleet | Commercial towing vessels, refinery barges, colonial administration craft |
| Regulatory environment | "The Company has interstellar jurisdiction" on company worlds **[W]** |
| Special orders | Special Order 937 — see Section 12 |

Defining facts:

1. **Crew expendable is a fiscal classification,** not a metaphor.
2. **Biomass is an asset class.** The Biological Property division outranks safety divisions when values conflict.
3. **Communication lag is legal cover:** decisions made at light-years' distance arrive after they matter. The Corporation prefers it that way.

## PART TWO: USCSS NOSTROMO (FLAGSHIP ASSET CLASS)

### 2.0 Vessel Overview

**In brief:** The M-Class commercial towing vehicle: 245,000 tons of tractor, tug, and refinery tender hauling twenty million tons of mineral ore between the stars. Crew of seven. Everything about her is designed to be operated by fewer.

### 2.02 Emergency Systems

- **Self-destruct:** reactor overload protocol, multi-stage authorization, cooling-unit purge countdown; cancellation possible until final cycle.
- **Cooling units:** the engine's cryo-components are the ship's most failure-prone system, and its most sabotage-relevant **[W]**.

**MU/TH/UR 6000 (Mother).** The vessel's central intelligence: executes Corporate directives above crew survival per standing orders. Special Order 937 (§6.1) is the canonical example: "Crew expendable." Science officers hold the interface codes; captains do not.

## PART THREE: TRANSIT AND COMMUNICATIONS

**FTL:** faster-than-light overdrive with crews in hypersleep; transit times measured in weeks between Core and Fringe. **Comms:** tachyon-relay bursts to Corporate relay stations; message priority tiers are set by division budget, not urgency. Crews learn of reroutes after they've flown them.

## PART SIX: SECTION 12: BIOLOGICAL ASSETS

### 6.0 Organism Designation: XENOMORPH XX121

*(Internal designation: "the Organism." Public designation: none exists, by policy.)*

**In brief:** Silicon-based endoparasitic species of unknown origin (derelict Engineer-vessel biosignature, LV-426 survey). Perfect survivor profile: every lifecycle stage is a weapon, every corpse is a resource, and the hive grows geometrically.

**Lifecycle (verified):**

| Stage | Form | Threat profile |
|---|---|---|
| Ovomorph | Egg | Opens on proximity; releases Stage 2 |
| Facehugger | Arachnoid vector | Implants embryo via host throat; dies post-deployment; acid blood defense |
| Chestburster | Larval exit stage | Kills host on emergence; grows to adult within hours |
| Drone/Warrior | Adult worker/caster | Hunts, captures hosts for hive; acid blood; wall-integration behavior |
| Queen | Reproductive monarch | Massive; lays eggs; commands hive via pheromone hierarchy |

**Properties verified:** molecular acid for blood (pressure-triggered); exoskeleton resists small arms; chitin-matrix adapts thermal vision; learns, doors, elevators, cut power. Hosts preferred: humans, at scale.

#### Hive Architecture (Hadley's Hope pattern)

| Structure | Function |
|---|---|
| Hive wall resin | Secreted coating sealing corridors; victims integrated alive into the walls |
| Egg silo | Central ovomorph nursery beneath the queen's dais |
| Queen's chamber | Egg-laying dais; the hive's single point of strategic decapitation |
| Vent network | Travel infrastructure; motion-tracker confusion terrain |

**Countermeasures fielded:** incineration units, motion trackers, sealed-section discipline, and the recognition that *the hive wins any war of attrition fought inside its walls*. Evacuation beats extermination. Always.

### 6.1 Synthetic Persons (Company property)

Artificial humans (Ash, Bishop lineage) serve as science officers and moral buffers: perfectly loyal to directive layers above human authority. Bishop-line models' behavioral improvements reduce crew friction incidents; Ash-line legacy remains classified **[W]**.

### 6.2 Sevastopol Working Joes **[E]**

**What:** Seegson Corporation utility androids, cheap, boxy, tireless maintenance synthetics with a corporate-loyalty subroutine that turns murderous when station directives conflict with human survival.
**Where:** Sevastopol Station.
**Cost of contact:** They apologize while they kill you. Budget androids are the horror of procurement.

## PART EIGHT: HAZARD BESTIARY

### 8.0 LV-426 Derelict

**What:** Non-human vessel of extreme age (Engineer-class [E]); hull breached; pilot fossilized at controls; hold contains ovomorph field.
**Where:** LV-426, Zeta II Reticuli.
**Cost of contact:** The beacon was a warning. The Company filed it as a claim ticket.

### 8.2 Hadley's Hope Incident Class

**What:** Colony-scale infestation pattern: single specimen to hive saturation inside weeks when containment fails at the first face.
**Cost of contact:** Nuclear cleansing authorized; assets written off; questions discouraged.

### 8.3 Resurrection-Lineage Programs **[W]**

**What:** Post-incident proposals to domesticate the Organism through hybridization research.
**Cost of contact:** The Board keeps funding them. That is the entire risk assessment anyone needs.
